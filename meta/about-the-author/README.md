# About the Author

*The personal layer. The why behind the world.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the author's reflections on the Sir Whiskerton Universe—why it was built, what it means, and how it was written. These are not canonical stories. They are not rules. They are not fan works.

They are the author's notes to the world.

---

## What Lives Here

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **01-why-i-built-this-world.md** | The personal reason for creating the universe. |
| **02-my-writing-philosophy.md** | The author's reflections on prose, on poetry, and on the writer's craft. |
| **03-the-farm-as-sanctuary.md** | The therapeutic purpose of the universe. |
| **04-letters-to-future-readers.md** | Fragments from the blog that speak directly to readers. |

---

## A Note on Reading These Documents

You do not need to read this folder to enjoy the farm. But if you want to understand *why* the farm exists—why Sir Whiskerton, why the hens, why the tygers sleeping in the bamboo grove—this is where the answer lives.

These documents are honest. They are sometimes painful. They are always true.
