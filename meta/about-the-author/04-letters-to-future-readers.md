# Letters to Future Readers

This is my universe. It is my creation, and I made it for people like us.

Everyone fights hidden and secret battles. Sometimes they bleed out and become visible, but most of the time we live with them; situations that we find ourselves trapped inside. Situations that we cannot escape from. Situations that we don't like, but live with because we seemingly have no other options.

Sometimes it's work. Sometimes it's friends. Sometimes it's our closest and dearest people near us.

And sometimes, the angst and hurt is far too deep to bear; to endure, and to suffer though.

And sometimes, it's everything.

Everything.

Maybe there's times when you just wish that you could hop into a time machine and go back to a simpler time; a time when the weight of life, and the burdens that you carry do not exist.

I remember.

It was raining out. My mother took off my yellow slicker, wet and glimmering with the sheen of rain, and wiped my face dry with a towel. Set me down at the kitchen table. And gave me a bowl of tomato soup. And with it was grilled cheese sandwich, with the brown-yellow bread, and gooey yellow cheese, melting at the sides. She taught me to dip it in the soup, and enjoy the taste of the tomato soup and gooey cheese ensemble.

Then, after I was finished, she made me a nice mug of hot chocolate. White tiny marshmallows floating at the top. And as it cooled, the surface formed a paper thin layer. Oh, it tasted good and swell in my stomach. Oh for certain, the rain continued to fall outside, but I was warm inside and I was loved. Loved.

I belonged.

I remember.

The world can be a harsh place. And everyone seemingly has an agenda. They take what they want, and in many cases, they are experts at this. One way or the other. And often you might find yourself alone. Surprised and a little stunned.

Don't be.

This place is for you.

I don't expect anything from you except "presence". You can be here, browse. Have fun, and even contribute. You are accepted just the way you are.

When you enter this world, accept Martha's pie, and feel the soft breeze on your cheek. You belong here. You know it. I know it, and the entire farm knows it.

Welcome.

Jingle on.
