# Provenance

*The record of how the universe came to be.*

---

## Purpose

This folder documents the process behind the Sir Whiskerton Universe. The drafts. The discarded versions. The conversations. The wrong turns. The moments of insight that arrived at 3 a.m.

Most creative works only show the surface—the finished story, the published book. They hide the process. The Sir Whiskerton Universe is different. It honors the process.

---

## What Lives Here

| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **/conversations/** | Selected conversations that shaped the universe. |
| **/drafts/** | Early drafts of stories, characters, and rules. |
| **/discarded/** | Ideas that were tried and set aside. |
| **/timeline.md** | A chronological record of how the universe evolved. |

---

## Why This Matters

The story is not just the story. The story is also the story of how the story came to be. This folder preserves that second story.

It is a gift to future readers, writers, and collaborators. It says: *The farm was not handed down from the mountaintop. It was built, brick by brick, through care and iteration and love.*
