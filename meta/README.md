# Meta

*The layers behind the story.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the documents that are not canon, not fan works, and not teaching materials. They are the personal layer—the reflections, the provenance, the letters to the future.

It is the human heart behind the universe.

## What Lives Here

| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **/about-the-author/** | The personal reflections. Why this world was built. What it means. How it was written. |
| **/provenance/** | The record of how the universe came to be. The discarded drafts. The conversations. The strata. |

## Why This Folder Exists

Most creative works only show the surface—the finished story, the published book, the completed project. They hide the process: the doubts, the wrong turns, the moments of insight that arrived at 3 a.m.

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is different. It honors the process. It preserves the debris. It says:

> *The story is not just the story. The story is also the story of how the story came to be.*

## A Note on Reading These Documents

You do not need to read this folder to enjoy the farm. But if you want to understand *why* the farm exists—why Sir Whiskerton, why the valley chicks, why the tygers sleeping in the bamboo grove—this is where the answer lives.
