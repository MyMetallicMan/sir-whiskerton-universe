# The Rolling Fields & Shadowy Woods: An Index of Transient & Peripheral Fauna

**Document Classification:** For Authorial Reference & Future Plot Bunnies

**Purpose:** To catalog the myriad of non-core animals who have passed through or reside on the periphery of the farm, providing a ready-made roster for guest appearances, plot catalysts, and world-building.

## Mammals

### Angus the Armadillo (流浪穿山甲 - "Wandering Pangolin")

**Status:** Transient (Annual Visitor).

**Profile:** A nomadic, resourceful hobo who travels from farm to farm asking for food. Rolls into a ball when flustered. His annual arrival is a source of amusement and minor mooching.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Rolling away from a conversation with Lucile the Parakeet.

**Reintro Hook:** He arrives with tall tales from other farms, or needing help with a problem he "rolled into."

### Bandit the Raccoon (强盗浣熊 - "Bandit Raccoon")

**Status:** Former Antagonist, Now Conditional Ally.

**Profile:** The infamous leader of a dangerous raccoon gang from the ancient forest, known for his handlebar mustache whiskers. Sly but ultimately good-hearted.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Living on the farm after Sir Whiskerton's intervention, but future potential for backsliding into trouble.

**Reintro Hook:** His old gang returns to pull him into "one last heist," or a new rival gang encroaches on his territory.

### Banjo the Monkey

**Status:** Transient (Runaway Circus Performer).

**Profile:** A mischievous, chaotic, and joyful monkey with a harmonica. A rule-breaker who brought laughter and camaraderie.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Brought balance to his chaos and became a beloved farm member.

**Reintro Hook:** His circus comes to town, forcing a reunion; he returns for a visit, bringing new circus-themed chaos.

### Buckle the Billy Goat (淘气登高羊 - "Mischievous Climbing Goat")

**Status:** Occasional Troublemaker.

**Profile:** An adventurous, stubborn goat with a love for climbing things he shouldn't (e.g., trees, rooftops).

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Rescued after getting stuck in a tree.

**Reintro Hook:** Gets stuck in a new, more precarious location (e.g., the farmer's weather vane, a precarious rock face).

### Chaz the Nervous Chipmunk (总总鼠 - "The Timid Mouse")

**Status:** Peripheral Resident.

**Profile:** Comically nervous, with wide, darting eyes and a single nut as a security blanket. Dives into his burrow at the slightest sound.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Hiding in his burrow.

**Reintro Hook:** A perfect character to discover a hidden threat or a secret by accident, if he can be coaxed out to tell about it.

### Humper the Rabbit (兔霸总 - "Rabbit CEO")

**Status:** Permanent Resident (with an enormous family).

**Profile:** A male rabbit who admires Catnip and leads a sea of 47 children. A walking population explosion.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Nibbling on anything green.

**Reintro Hook:** His family's insatiable appetite causes a food shortage; one of his many children gets lost or has a unique personality.

### Mary Hoppins (胡萝卜琳 - "Carrot-Lynn")

**Status:** Occasional Visitor / Educator.

**Profile:** A cheerful, nurturing, and wise rabbit modeled after Mary Poppins, who teaches the farm's young ones. Prim appearance, carries a parasol.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Having concluded a lesson.

**Reintro Hook:** Returns to teach a new batch of youngsters, or to help a specific character through a personal crisis with her whimsical wisdom.

### Porkchop the Pig (猪大排 - "Pork Chop")

**Status:** Permanent Resident.

**Profile:** A food-loving pig with a sarcastic streak and a hidden artistic talent. Good-natured and witty.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Considering a tattoo after meeting Lester the tattooed pig.

**Reintro Hook:** His artistic pursuits could lead him into a rivalry or partnership with Artist Agnes; his sarcasm could finally land him in real trouble.

### Simon the Hedgehog (慌张刺球 - "Panicky Pincushion")

**Status:** Permanent Resident.

**Profile:** A tiny, tenacious, and jittery hedgehog who is the self-appointed "eyes and ears" of the barnyard. Delivers urgent (and often exaggerated) news to Sir Whiskerton.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Scurrying somewhere at top speed.

**Reintro Hook:** The perfect character to stumble upon the beginning of a mystery. His "distressed noises" could finally be about a real, legitimate threat.

### Slow Bob the Turtle (慢存存 - "Super Slow")

**Status:** Permanent, Timeless Resident.

**Profile:** A 97-year-old turtle whose shell was painted by a young Pablo Picasso. He is wise, deliberate, and possesses the ability to time travel using his art-adorned shell.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Basking in the sun, contemplating eternity.

**Reintro Hook:** A time-travel plotline. He could take another character to a key moment in the farm's past (or future) to solve a problem.

## Birds (Non-Chicken/Goose/Duck)

### Cornelius the Crow (混世鸦王 - "Chaos Crow King")

**Status:** Agent of Chaos.

**Profile:** A feathered agent of chaos, a mischievous maestro of mayhem. Delights in petty theft, dive-bombing, and sowing turmoil.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Cackling from a rooftop as havoc unfolds below.

**Reintro Hook:** The perfect culprit for a series of petty crimes. His stolen "shiny objects" could include a crucial item.

### Edgar the Crow (恶霸鸦哥 / 忽悠鸦·诸葛喻 - "Bully Crow Brother" / "Hustle Crow - Dark Zhuge")

**Status:** Fearless Intimidator.

**Profile:** A bold and brazen crow that even the scarecrow fears. A potential trickster figure or protector.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Asserting dominance somewhere.

**Reintro Hook:** Could be hired (or bribed) for aerial reconnaissance; his fearlessness could be the key to scaring off a new threat.

### Elvis the Rooster (猫王蛋总 - "Cat King Egg Boss")

**Status:** Former Transient, now Legend.

**Profile:** A flamboyant rockstar rooster, a self-proclaimed reincarnation of the King. Caused a stir by attempting to lay an egg to break biological norms.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Strutting away after his dramatic performance.

**Reintro Hook:** Returns for a comeback tour, challenging Ferdinand and Lady Quacka for musical supremacy.

### Polly the Peacock (傲娇孔雀·鹅力鸭 - "Proud Peacock · Goose-Duck")

**Status:** Permanent Resident.

**Profile:** A flamboyant, self-important peacock who mistakenly believed it was a goose. Dramatic and full of flair.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Causing chaos with colorful displays.

**Reintro Hook:** An identity crisis when it meets real peacocks; its feathers could be the target of a thief.

### Sedgwick the Barn Owl (优郁大师 - "Master of Gloom")

**Status:** Nocturnal Observer.

**Profile:** A gloomy, melancholic owl who offers pessimistic but often accurate observations from the rafters.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Watching silently from the barn beams.

**Reintro Hook:** The perfect "gloomy oracle" to provide a dire warning that everyone initially ignores.

## Reptiles & Amphibians

### Leonardo the Bullfrog (雄霸天 - "Frog Overlord")

**Status:** Permanent Resident.

**Profile:** A bullfrog with a booming voice and big ambitions to create a new frog-led governance for the farm. Ultimately learned to be himself.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Composing odes to mud puddles as the farm's resident poet.

**Reintro Hook:** His ambitions could be rekindled; his powerful croak could be used as a secret weapon or signal.

## Invertebrates & Others

### The Cockroach Trio (蟑螂三侠 - "The Cockroach Trio")

**Members:** Ping (乒乓), Dot (点点), Splatter (泼墨).

**Status:** Retired Circus Performers.

**Profile:** Former stars of The Amazing Cockroach Circus, now challenging barnyard animals to hopscotch duels. Ping is the "brains," Dot is the artist, Splatter is the slapstick comedian.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** Performing nightly in the hayloft (admission: one corn kernel).

**Reintro Hook:** Their circus past comes back to haunt them; they attempt a grand, new death-defying stunt that goes horribly (and hilariously) wrong.

### Snailbert the Snail (蜗牛伯特 - "Snailbert")

**Status:** Pre-Dawn Philosopher.

**Profile:** A snail who paints with a single drop of blue paint on the dawn grass, believing it is "the sky's." Moves so slowly he is eternal.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** On an infinite journey across the dew-laden grass.

**Reintro Hook:** One of his paintings holds a hidden message or map, visible only from a certain angle at sunrise.

### The Yodeling Fish (唱歌鱼 - "Singing Fish")

**Status:** Permanent Pond Residents.

**Profile:** A trio (sometimes more) of fish that yodel ("YODEL-AY-HEE-HOO!") in synchronized, 1940s-era style. Their yodeling has a hypnotic effect on all who hear it.

**Last Known Whereabouts:** In the pond, potentially the result of one of Chef Remy's failed experiments.

**Reintro Hook:** Their hypnotic power could be used to resolve a conflict—or be weaponized by a villain. A visiting marine biologist could be utterly baffled by them.

## How to Use This Index

1. **The Guest Star:** Pick a character for a one-off appearance to spice up an episode (e.g., Chaz the Chipmunk freaking out over nothing).
2. **The Plot Driver:** Use a character's unique trait to initiate a story (e.g., Slow Bob time-travels, the Yodeling Fish hypnotize the farm).
3. **The Returning Antagonist:** Bring back a former foe like Bandit, but with a new angle or a shared threat that forces an alliance.
4. **The Thematic Mirror:** Use a character to highlight a theme. Snailbert can teach a lesson about patience, while the Cockroach Trio can show that everyone has value.

This index ensures that no interesting character is ever truly lost, providing a deep bench of personalities to enrich the world of Sir Whiskerton.
