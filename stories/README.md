## How the Stories Are Organized

### The Four Levels

**1. The Collection**

A collection is a complete thematic arc — a big-picture exploration of a single idea, told through many stories. Each collection has:

- A **name** (e.g., "The Jingle-Jangle Farm")
- A **core theme** (e.g., "The Architecture of Belonging")
- A **README** explaining what the collection is
- A **thematic analysis** exploring how it works
- One or more **volumes** within it

**2. The Volume**

A volume is a thematic grouping within a collection. Each volume has:

- A **name** (e.g., "Tales from the Junkyard Playground")
- A **core theme** (e.g., "New Arrivals & First Lessons")
- A **README** explaining what the volume is and listing its stories
- Multiple **stories** within it

**3. The Story**

A story is a single tale from the farm. Each story is a `.md` file — fully written out, formatted for reading, and annotated with:

- The story itself
- A **moral** summary
- **Best lines**
- A **post-credit scene**
- **Key jokes**
- A **starring** list of characters
- A **P.S.** with some wisdom from the farm

**4. The Character Files**

Every character who appears in the stories has their own full bio, filed separately under [`characters/`](../characters/). The stories reference these bios; the bios describe the characters who live in the stories.

## The Story Format

Every story in the Sir Whiskerton Universe follows the same format:

- **A title** — always beginning with "Sir Whiskerton and..." (usually)
- **The story itself** — 2,400 words minimum, sometimes up to 4,000
- **The moral** — the lesson the story is meant to teach
- **Best lines** — the moments that shine brightest
- **A post-credit scene** — a light coda to the story
- **Key jokes** — the puns, the wordplay, the comedic moments
- **Starring** — the major characters, with their roles described humorously
- **A P.S.** — some final wisdom from the farm

This format is intentional. It turns every story into a self-contained reading *and* teaching experience.

*(For the full rules, see [the General Story Rules](../writing-guidelines/general_story_rules.md).)*

## A Note on the Tone

The stories in this repository are the *heart* of the Sir Whiskerton Universe. They are:

- **Gentle** — quiet stakes, soft humor, no cruelty
- **Whimsical** — the farm's absurdity is a feature, not a bug
- **Warm** — even the sad moments are held
- **Earnest** — no irony for irony's sake; sincerity is the point
- **Soft** — nothing truly dies; things change form

Every story should leave the reader feeling a little warmer, a little lighter, and a little more at home.

*(For the full philosophy, see [the Charter of the Farmstead](../writing-guidelines/the_charter_of_the_farmstead.md).)*
