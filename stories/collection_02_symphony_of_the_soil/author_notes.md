## A Note Before We Begin

Collection 1's Author's Notes were about *how the stories came to be*.

Collection 2's Author's Notes are about something *harder*. They are about *why the stories had to be written*.

Most of the stories in this collection were written during a period of personal chaos. I was between jobs — *repeatedly*, in a world that had stopped treating professionals as anything other than disposable. I was watching a career I had trained for dissolve, and rebuilding something *quieter* in its place. And in the middle of all of that, I was writing about a farm.

The farm was not a *distraction*. It was a *life raft*. And these notes are the *backstories* — the small, true, sometimes painful things that *prompted* each story. They are not *explanations*. They are *origins*.

I've written them in the first person, because they are mine. They are not meant to be literature. They are meant to be *the truth behind the fable*. Some are *grand*. Some are *mundane*. Both kinds *matter*.

— Robert

## Story 2A003 — Sir Whiskerton and the Great Toilet Paper Shortage

This story was written during COVID.

I was in China. The pandemic had emptied the streets, closed the schools, and — for a few strange months — made the entire world *very, very quiet*. And I was watching, from a continent away, as my home country went to war over *toilet paper*.

Not rice. Not water. Not canned goods. Not medicine. **Toilet paper.**

I remember thinking: *this is the funniest thing I have ever seen*. And also: *this is the most revealing thing I have ever seen*.
