# Sir Whiskerton and the Arrival of Super-Squirrel

*Previously cataloged as 5C108*

## Foreword

Ah, dear reader. You have returned, ready for a tale of physics, protein, and profound spatial unawareness. Tonight’s story is not one of malice, but of momentum—of what happens when a creature is given the strength of a draft horse and the impulse control of a caffeinated toddler. 

Do not be alarmed. This is simply Tuesday on the farm: the day a squirrel bench-pressed a boulder, a barn was nearly lifted off its foundation, and Sir Whiskerton reminded everyone—with one velvet paw on a splintered beam—that true strength isn't measured in pounds lifted, but in the quiet grace of a job well done.

## I. The Seismic Rodent

It all began on a quiet, serene morning. I was enjoying a peaceful nap on the Farmer’s porch, the sun warm on my fur, the world perfectly still. 

Then, a loud, earth-shaking *CRASH* shattered the tranquility. 

I bolted upright, my fur standing on end, my monocle slipping a fraction of an inch. “What in the name of catnip was that?” I muttered, my ears twitching in irritation.

I leapt down from the porch and made my way to the source of the noise: the center of the barnyard. There, standing in a cloud of dust and displaced topsoil, was a squirrel. He was holding an entire, fully grown oak sapling over his head with one paw. 

“Behold!” the squirrel declared, his voice booming with unnatural resonance. “I am Super-Squirrel, the strongest squirrel in the world!”

The farm animals gathered around, their eyes wide with a mixture of amazement and profound terror. 

“That’s not a squirrel,” Doris the Hen clucked, her feathers ruffled, her clipboard trembling. “That’s a bulldozer with a tail! And he’s got muscles I’ve never seen before!”

I approached the squirrel, my green eyes narrowing. “Super-Squirrel, is it? And what, pray tell, brings you to our humble, structurally vulnerable farm?”

Super-Squirrel grinned, flexing his impressive, bulging biceps. “I’ve come to show the world my strength! And what better place to start than a farm? I shall prove my power!”

## II. The Chaos of Gains

Super-Squirrel’s arrival quickly turned the farm upside down. His super-strength, while undeniably impressive, was not exactly… controlled. 

He uprooted three perfectly good trees to build a “super nest” that was entirely too heavy for the branches to hold. He flipped over hay bales just to “test his limits,” scattering alfalfa across three counties. He even tried to lift the barn, wedging his shoulders under the foundation and grunting, just to “see if he could.”

“This is getting out of hand,” I said, flicking my tail in irritation as a shower of dirt rained down on my kilt. “We need to find a way to help Super-Squirrel control his powers before he destroys the very farm he’s trying to impress.”

The first step was to enlist the help of Nutters the Squirrel, the farm’s resident street-smart rodent and self-proclaimed leader of the local squirrel gang. 

“Nutters,” I said, finding him watching Super-Squirrel from the safety of a high fencepost. “We need your expertise. Super-Squirrel is causing chaos, and we need to help him channel his strength.”

Nutters, who had been watching the antics with a mixture of awe and deep skepticism, nodded slowly. “I’ll see what I can do, Whiskerton. But no promises—this guy is strong. And he’s got a lot to prove.”

## III. The Intervention of Nutters

Nutters approached Super-Squirrel, who was currently bench-pressing a massive, moss-covered boulder near the pond. 

“Hey, big guy,” Nutters said, his tone casual, leaning against a pebble. “Nice muscles. Very vascular. But you know, strength isn’t just about lifting heavy things.”

Super-Squirrel paused mid-lift, the boulder hovering inches from his chest. His brow furrowed. “What do you mean? Isn’t that… the whole point?”

“I mean,” Nutters said, hopping up onto the boulder and sitting comfortably, “you could use your strength to help others instead of just showing off. Like, say, helping us gather acorns for the winter before the frost hits. Or building a better, draft-free nest for the birds. Stuff like that. Real strength is about service, man.”

Super-Squirrel thought for a moment. The boulder gently lowered to the ground with a soft *thud*. 

“You’re right,” Super-Squirrel said, his voice softer, less booming. “I’ve been so focused on proving how strong I am that I forgot why I have these powers in the first place. I want to help!”

## IV. The Channeling of the Gains

With Nutters’s guidance, Super-Squirrel began to learn how to use his strength responsibly. 

They started with small tasks. Gathering acorns, which Super-Squirrel could do by the bushel in seconds. Repairing the chicken coop, which had been leaning precariously since the Great Wind of '22. 

“See?” Nutters said, as Super-Squirrel carefully, gently placed a heavy oak beam back into place, his massive paws moving with surprising delicacy. “This is what real strength looks like. It’s not about how much you can lift—it’s about how much you can help.”

Super-Squirrel grinned, his muscles flexing as he secured the beam. “This feels amazing! I’m actually making a difference!”

The farm animals, initially wary of the destructive rodent, began to warm up to him. Doris the Hen even offered him a few unsolicited, highly specific tips on nest-building. 

“You’ve got to make it sturdy,” she clucked, tapping the new beams with her wing. “But also cozy. Good airflow, but no drafts. No one wants to sleep in a cold, drafty nest, Super-Squirrel. Ergonomics are key!”

Super-Squirrel listened intently, nodding, applying his immense power to the delicate art of avian interior design.

## The Moral (and the Post-Credit Whisper)

**Moral:**  
Strength is best used to help others, not to show off. Whether you’re a squirrel with super-strength, a cat with a knack for solving mysteries, or a pig with a love for food, it’s important to use your talents for the greater good. True strength isn't about the weight you can move; it's about the weight you can lift off someone else's shoulders.

As the sun set over the farm, the animals reflected on the day’s events. The uprooted trees had been replanted, the hay bales were neatly stacked, and the barn was safe once more. Super-Squirrel, now a beloved member of the farm community, sat quietly on a branch, gently cracking an acorn for a baby bird.

As for Nutters, he took pride in his role as Super-Squirrel’s mentor. “Who knew I’d be teaching a superhero?” he said, puffing out his chest. “Guess I’m stronger than I thought.”

**Post-Credit Scene:**  
Later that week, the Farmer was struggling to open a particularly stubborn jar of pickles. He grunted, he strained, he tapped the lid. Nothing.

Super-Squirrel, passing by, gently took the jar from the Farmer’s hands. With a soft, precise twist, the lid popped open. He handed it back, gave a polite nod, and scurried up the nearest tree.

The Farmer stared at the open jar, then up at the tree. “Well,” he murmured. “I’ll be.”

I watched from the porch, sipping my cream. “Indeed, Farmer,” I purred. “Indeed.”
