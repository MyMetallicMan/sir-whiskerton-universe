# Tales by Firefly

A collection of ten quiet, luminous stories set on and around a small farm at the edge of a pasture — a place where a dragon believes he is a cat, a croc-a-gator believes she is a dog, a genie navigates by vibe, and a beatnik cat plays bongo drums for a jade ghost who may or may not be watching. These are tales of belonging without earning, joy as resistance, and the calm persistence of creatures who have decided to make their own fit.

## The Farm and Its Inhabitants

**The Farm** sits at the edge of a pasture, bounded by a wood, a pond, and a long green ribbon of abandoned railway — the **Long Road** — whose rusted rails remember where they're going. It is a place governed by a single, unspoken rule: *nothing dies; things change form.* It is a soft place, a holding place, a place where the lost are simply late.

### The Residents

- **Longwei the Dragon (龙喵)** — A dragon the size of a small bus who is utterly convinced he is a cat. He purrs, naps in sunbeams, and has a weakness for dumplings. He is the farm's gentle heart.
- **Sir Whiskerton (胡子爵士)** — The monocled cat-detective and quiet witness. He does not solve mysteries so much as honor them. He watches, and in watching, holds the farm together.
- **Catnip the Stray Cat (坏蛋猫)** — The sovereign shadow, once a house cat named Clement. Cynical, calculating, and secretly tender. He protects what he loves without ever admitting he loves it.
- **Esmeralda the Croc-a-Gator (沼心鳄)** — A bayou-born croc-a-gator raised by her Mémé to "make her own fit." She believes she is a dog, plays fetch with abandon, and sings Cajun lullabies to frightened hens.
- **Jazzpurr the Beatnik Cat (爵士猫)** — The farm's resident poet and bongo drummer. He finds the rhythm in everything and plays quiet rhythms for ghosts who may or may not be watching.
- **Zephyr the Genie (扎菲尔)** — A vibe-based navigator who does not believe in maps ("super oppressive to the concept of *yonder*"). He conjures flying carpets and speaks in the language of frequencies.
- **Count Catula (吸血鬼猫)** — A gothic romantic cat whose cape is, regrettably, not rated for high altitudes. He is undone by practical aerodynamics and sustained by drama.
- **The Farmer** — The quiet heart of the farm. He does not demand that anyone stay within the fences; he only asks that they come back.
- **Martha** — The farmer's wife, who leaves second bowls of cream on the windowsill before anyone asks, and who never needed to be told.
- **Doris the Hen, Harriet, Lillian, Gertrude the Goose, Porkchop the Pig, Bessie the Tie-Dye Cow, Rufus the Dog** — The farm's chorus. They gossip, they faint, they file manifestos, and they witness.
- **Ratso the Rat** — A trench-coated noir narrator who is learning that the coat is a costume, and that the truth is simpler and scarier: he wants to be loved as he is.
- **Echo the Kitten** — The one who sleeps like she's never had a reason not to. She knows what Ratso is, and she lets him keep the coat on anyway.
- **Old Mable** — The ancient tree at the edge of the pasture, carved with the initials *J.M. + E.R.* She is the farm's memory-keeper and does not speak, but her leaves rustle like eucalyptus on a road in a country no one has seen.
- **An Yi the Dragon (安逸)** — Longwei's oldest friend, who came to say goodbye and left on the evening breeze.
- **Long Yi** — Longwei's sister, a dragon of the Eastern Skies who visits secretly under full moons and speaks only to the wind.
- **Chef Remy LeRaccoon & Dr. Notorious** — A half-crazed raccoon chef and his white lab rat assistant, working on vibrating green jelly in the tool shed.
- **The Rabbits of the Sunken Glade** — Who watch from their warrens, who do not approach, and who know what to do with a shell after the fire has gone out.

## Themes

These stories are built on a handful of recurring convictions:

- **Belonging without earning** — You do not have to prove you deserve a place here. The second bowl is already on the windowsill.
- **Joy as resistance** — Purring, wagging, playing fetch, and writing poems are acts of defiance against a world that expects you to be practical.
- **Calm persistence** — The farm does not rush. The Long Road does not waver. Things happen in their own time.
- **The absurd is sacred** — A dragon who is a cat. A croc-a-gator who is a dog. A yodeling pond. These are not jokes; they are sacraments.
- **Change of form, not death** — The farm's soft place rule. Those we love do not leave us; they become smoke on the evening breeze, fire in the memory, a shell the earth knows how to hold.
- **Presence as the only gift** — To witness is enough. To stay is enough. To lie down beside someone in the dark is enough.
- **Love as a road, not a ring** — A gift that outlives the giver. A grove planted for strangers. A poem written for a ghost.
