# The Steward's Charter

**For the Sir Whiskerton Universe**

*Ratified by the Steward on the date of this repository's creation.*

---

## Preamble

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is a world of belonging, joy, calm persistence, and unconditional acceptance. It was built as a sanctuary—a place where the discarded become oracles, where ancient trees hold memory, where the road leads somewhere, and where every creature, no matter how absurd or broken, finds a home.

This Charter exists to protect that sanctuary. It is not a legal contract in the traditional sense. It is a covenant. It is a promise made by the Steward to the world, and by the world to the Steward.

It says: *This is not for sale. This is not for acquisition. This is not a franchise. This is a legacy.*

---

## Article I: The Steward

**1.1** The Sir Whiskerton Universe is stewarded by its creator, hereafter referred to as "the Steward."

**1.2** The Steward holds final authority over what is considered canonical and what is not.

**1.3** The Steward's vision for the universe—its themes, its characters, its tone, and its moral center—is the foundation upon which all decisions are made.

**1.4** The Steward may, at any time, clarify, expand, or refine the canon. But the Steward may never sell, transfer, or surrender ownership of the universe to any individual, corporation, or entity.

---

## Article II: The Core Themes

**2.1** The Sir Whiskerton Universe is built on the following core themes. These are not suggestions. They are the soil from which every story grows.

- **Belonging Without Earning.** You do not audition for a place here. You arrive—scruffy, scarred, or shining—and find the door was already open.
- **Joy as Resistance.** In a world that profits from sorrow and haste, choosing joy is a revolutionary act.
- **Calm Persistence (静韧).** Not force, not speed, but steady, gentle return. The greatest changes come not with a bang, but with a purr, a pat, a patient presence that never wavers.
- **The Afterlife of Objects.** Nothing is ever truly lost or broken. A cracked bowl mended with gold holds beauty, not perfection. Objects transition from function, through a breaking point, into meaning.
- **The Economics of Joy.** Joy is renewable, compounding capital. The currency is care: a purr, a shared silence, a button offered without reason.
- **Vulnerability as the Only Door.** The bravest act on the farm is to say, "Here I am—messy, afraid, real. Do you have room for me?" And the farm always answers: "Yes. Come in."

**2.2** Any work that contradicts, undermines, or mocks these themes is not authorized.

---

## Article III: The Canon

**3.1** The canonical stories, character bios, artifact documents, and worldbuilding notes are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)**.

**3.2** This means:
- Anyone may share the canon with proper credit to the Steward.
- No one may use the canon for commercial purposes.
- No one may alter, remix, or create derivatives of the canonical texts.

**3.3** The canon is the sacred text of the universe. It remains as the Steward wrote it.

---

## Article IV: Fan Works

**4.1** Fans are welcomed and encouraged to create within the Sir Whiskerton Universe.

**4.2** Fan works are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**.

**4.3** This means fan works must:
- Credit the Steward.
- Be shared freely (not sold).
- Carry the same license (ShareAlike).
- Respect the core themes.
- Not claim to be official or canonical.

**4.4** Fan works are **not canon**. They are gifts to the community. They live in the `/fan-works/` folder, clearly separated from the canon.

**4.5** The Steward reserves the right to request that any fan work be removed if it:
- Violates the core themes.
- Promotes hate, violence, or cruelty.
- Misrepresents the universe as official or canonical.
- Uses the universe for commercial gain.

---

## Article V: Commercial Use

**5.1** No commercial use of the Sir Whiskerton Universe is permitted without explicit written agreement from the Steward.

**5.2** This includes, but is not limited to:
- Merchandise for sale.
- Crowdfunded projects.
- Adaptations (film, television, games, audio dramas).
- AI training.
- Corporate use.
- Any use of the "Sir Whiskerton" name or related character names as branding.

**5.3** Personal, non-commercial merchandise (items made for oneself, for friends, or as gifts, not for sale) is permitted.

**5.4** Fan zines and similar community publications may be sold at cost (not for profit) with attribution.

---

## Article VI: The Protection

**6.1** The Sir Whiskerton Universe may not be acquired, purchased, or claimed by any individual or corporation.

**6.2** It is not for sale. It is not a franchise. It is not a brand to be exploited.

**6.3** No individual or entity may claim ownership of the universe, its characters, its stories, or its name.

**6.4** Any attempt to acquire the universe, whether through purchase, legal maneuver, or corporate takeover, is a violation of this Charter and is not authorized.

---

## Article VII: The Steward's Succession

**7.1** If the Steward dies or becomes incapacitated, the universe shall be:

- **Preserved as-is.** No one may alter the canon.
- **Made permanently available to the public** under the same terms as this Charter.
- **Entrusted to a designated successor** if one is named by the Steward in a separate document.
- **Left to the community to preserve, not to own.** The community may maintain the repository and share the work, but may not sell, alter, or claim ownership.

**7.2** The universe is a legacy. It is not a possession to be inherited. It is a gift to be protected.

---

## Article VIII: The Door Is Open

**8.1** Everyone is welcome here. This is a place of belonging.

**8.2** Come as you are. Stay as long as you like. Leave when you must.

**8.3** But do not try to take this place from those it belongs to. It belongs to all of us.

**8.4** The farm is not a fortress. It is a home. And homes are meant to be shared.

---

## Ratification

This Charter is ratified by the Steward on the date of this repository's creation. It is a living document. It may be amended by the Steward, but it may never be revoked.

It says, clearly and permanently: *This world is not for sale. It is not for acquisition. It is a legacy.*
