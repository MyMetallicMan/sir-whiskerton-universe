# Allowed Fan Merchandise

*What fans can make without permission.*

---

## Purpose

The Sir Whiskerton Universe welcomes fan-created merchandise that is personal, non-commercial, or educational. This document defines what is allowed without seeking permission from the Steward.

---

## Allowed Without Permission

### Personal Items

- T-shirts, mugs, stickers, and other items made **for yourself**
- Items made **for friends or family** as gifts
- Cosplay costumes and props for personal use

### Educational Use

- Classroom materials, worksheets, and activities
- Educational handouts and reader guides
- School projects and student-created art

### Fan Zines and Non-Commercial Publications

- Fan zines sold **at cost** (materials + minimal printing costs, not for profit)
- Free digital publications shared with the community
- Library displays and community events

### Digital Creations

- Fan art shared freely online
- Fan fiction shared freely online
- Fan-made animations, comics, and audio content
- Fan wikis and reference materials

---

## The Spirit

The farm is a place of belonging. Fan merchandise is an act of love—a way of saying *"I want to carry this world with me."* The rules exist to protect that love, not to restrict it.

If you are unsure whether your project is allowed, ask yourself:

> *"Would this feel like a gift to the farm, or would it feel like an extraction from the farm?"*

If it is a gift, it is likely allowed.

---

## A Note on Attribution

Even for personal items, we encourage you to credit the Sir Whiskerton Universe as the inspiration. This is not required, but it is a way of honoring the source.

> *Based on the Sir Whiskerton Universe by Congjing Yu.*
