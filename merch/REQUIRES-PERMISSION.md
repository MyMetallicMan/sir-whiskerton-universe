# Merchandise Requiring Permission

*What fans and creators must ask before making.*

---

## Purpose

Some merchandise exists in a gray zone between "fan work" and "commercial product." This document defines what requires written permission from the Steward before it can be created.

---

## Requires Written Permission

### Commercial Merchandise

- Any item sold for profit (beyond cost of materials)
- Mass-produced items (T-shirts, mugs, posters, etc.) for sale
- Merchandise sold through online marketplaces (Etsy, Redbubble, etc.)
- Any use of the "Sir Whiskerton" name as a brand

### Crowdfunded Projects

- Kickstarter, Patreon, Ko-fi, and similar platforms
- Any project where money is raised in advance
- Any project where the Sir Whiskerton Universe is the primary benefit

### Adaptations

- Animated films, television shows, or web series
- Audio dramas and podcasts
- Video games and interactive media
- Stage plays and live performances
- Any adaptation of the stories or characters

### AI Training and Generation

- Any use of the Sir Whiskerton Universe for AI training
- Any AI-generated content based on the universe
- Any use of the universe's text, images, or characters in AI systems

### Corporate Use

- Any use by a company, even for "brand awareness" or "internal inspiration"
- Any use in advertising or marketing
- Any use in corporate training materials

---

## How to Request Permission

If you would like to create something in this category, please:

1. **Open an issue on the GitHub repository** describing your project.
2. **Wait for a response from the Steward or a designated representative.**
3. **Do not proceed with your project until you have received written permission.**

The Steward reserves the right to grant or deny permission at their discretion. Permission may be granted with conditions (such as royalties, attribution requirements, or content restrictions).

---

## The Spirit

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is not for sale. It is a legacy. Commercial use is considered only when it serves the long-term protection and flourishing of the universe—not when it extracts from it.

If a project is aligned with the Charter, the Steward will consider it. If it is not, no amount of money will secure permission.
