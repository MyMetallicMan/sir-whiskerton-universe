# Never Allowed

*The lines that cannot be crossed.*

---

## Purpose

Some uses of the Sir Whiskerton Universe are categorically forbidden. This document defines those lines. No amount of money, no level of prestige, no promise of exposure can change them.

---

## Never Allowed

### Claiming Ownership

- Claiming to have created, own, or control the Sir Whiskerton Universe
- Registering the names, characters, or story elements as trademarks
- Filing copyright claims against the Steward or other fans
- Presenting fan work as official or canonical

### Creating "Official" Spin-Offs

- Any project that claims to be an "official" or "canonical" continuation
- Any project that presents itself as authorized without permission
- Any project that misrepresents its relationship to the universe

### Deviating from Core Themes

- Stories or works that promote cruelty, hatred, or violence
- Works that undermine the Doctrine of Belonging
- Works that invert the Sacred Functions of characters
- Works that mock or degrade the farm's values

### Harmful Uses

- Using the universe to promote hate, bigotry, or harassment
- Using the universe for political manipulation
- Using the universe for commercial exploitation of children
- Using the universe to cause harm to any person or group

### Corporate Acquisition

- Any attempt to acquire, purchase, or claim ownership of the universe
- Any attempt to license the universe for corporate control
- Any attempt to turn the universe into a "brand" or "franchise"

---

## The Consequences

Violation of these rules may result in:

1. **Immediate termination of any permission or license** previously granted
2. **Public disclosure** of the violation
3. **Legal action** if applicable
4. **Permanent exclusion** from the Sir Whiskerton community

The Steward is not obligated to issue warnings. Some lines, once crossed, cannot be uncrossed.

---

## Why These Lines Exist

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is a sanctuary. It is not a product. It is not a franchise. It is not a commodity to be bought, sold, or exploited.

These lines protect the sanctuary. They protect the readers. They protect the Steward. They protect the *idea* that there are some things that should not be for sale.
