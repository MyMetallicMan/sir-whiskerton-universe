# The ESL Framework

*The pedagogical architecture of the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the teaching framework for the Sir Whiskerton stories. It is designed for Chinese ESL learners aged 10–14, aligned with China's *Compulsory Education English Curriculum Standards (2022)*.

The stories are not just stories. They are modern fables with a complete bilingual teaching platform attached. Each book is roughly **35% narrative** and **65% teaching materials**—a ratio that ensures the story is a delightful read and a powerful teaching tool.

## The Approach

**Laugh first, learn second.** Humor disarms fear. Students may forget what they read, but they will remember how it made them feel—and that feeling carries the language with it.

**Grammar in disguise.** Tenses, modals, comparatives, and idioms are embedded in the narrative, then made explicit in the ESL sections.

**Progressive challenge.** Lessons are structured like game levels—each one slightly harder, each one rewarding.

## The Three Editions

Each Sir Whiskerton story exists in three parallel editions, calibrated to different proficiency levels:

| Edition | CEFR Level | Audience | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Foundations** | A2 | Beginners | Simple sentences, high-frequency vocabulary, clear moral |
| **Explorers** | B1 | Intermediate | Richer prose, more complex structures, same heart |
| **Author** | C1/C2 | Advanced | Literary, poetic, full voice of the original |

Each edition is a **re-creation**, not a translation. They are the same event seen through three different lenses.

## The Golden Rules

> **1. Laugh First, Learn Second.**
> Use humor to lower barriers. Memes over memorization. Jokes before jargon. Chuckles with chunks.

> **2. Grammar in Disguise.**
> Embed language concepts naturally. Stealthy tense transitions. Secret syntax sprinkles. Covert clause construction.

> **3. Progressive Challenge.**
> Structure lessons like game levels. +5 vocabulary XP. Boss battle conjugations. Final grammar dungeon.

## The Purpose

This is not just English class. It is a safe space for the quiet child, the overconfident student, the one who feels invisible. The stories say, in every language:

> *"You are seen."*
