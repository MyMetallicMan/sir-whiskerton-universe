# Super-Squirrel: The Mighty Hero of the Farm

**Title:** The Strongest Squirrel in the World / The Red-Caped Hero / The One Who Learned to Help

**Chinese Name:** 壮壮侠 (Zhuàng Zhuàng Xiá) — *"Mighty Hero"*

---

## ROLE ON THE FARM

Super-Squirrel is the farm's **resident superhero** and the **strongest squirrel in the world**. With muscles that could rival a bodybuilder's and a heart as big as his biceps, he is a force to be reckoned with.

He arrived on the farm in a whirlwind of chaos, uprooting trees and flipping hay bales with ease, all in the name of *showcasing his incredible strength*. But beneath his brawny exterior lies a squirrel with a genuine desire to help others — even if his initial attempts were a little... *destructive*.

With his bright red cape (made from an old handkerchief) and a can-do attitude, Super-Squirrel is a larger-than-life character who brings both power and heart to the farm.

---

## HIS JOURNEY

Super-Squirrel's journey is one of **growth and self-discovery**.

Initially focused on *proving* his strength, he learns that true power lies in **how you use it**. Under the guidance of **Nutters the Squirrel**, Super-Squirrel discovers the joy of helping others — from gathering acorns for the winter to repairing the chicken coop.

His transformation from a show-off to a selfless hero is a testament to the importance of humility and the value of teamwork. Whether he's lifting boulders or building nests, Super-Squirrel's strength is matched only by his determination to make a positive impact on the world around him.

---

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS

### Nutters the Squirrel — The Mentor

Nutters taught Super-Squirrel that true strength isn't about *showing off*. It's about *showing up*.

### Sir Whiskerton — The Observer

Sir Whiskerton keeps a close eye on Super-Squirrel. Not because he distrusts him, but because Super-Squirrel's *enthusiasm* often outpaces his *judgment*. Sir Whiskerton has, on more than one occasion, gently redirected a boulder-lift before it could become a barn-collapse.

### The Farm Animals — The Beneficiaries

The farm animals love Super-Squirrel. Not because he is the strongest — but because he is the *kindest*. He has never once used his strength for himself. He has always used it for *them*.
