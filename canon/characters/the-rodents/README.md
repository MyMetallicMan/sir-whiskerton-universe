# The Rodents

The underground society. The ones who live beneath.

## Purpose

This folder contains the rodent characters who operate in the shadows of the farm. They are the thieves, the dreamers, the enforcers, and the accidental philosophers. They are the farm's underworld, its complication, and its chaos.

They are not evil. They are hungry. They are ambitious. They are trying to survive in a world built for larger creatures.

They are the farm's mirror held low to the ground—where the crumbs fall, where the schemes hatch, and where the smallest creatures dream the biggest dreams.

## The Factions

The rodents are not one people. They are many. And they do not always get along.

### The Catnip Syndicate

The primary criminal organization operating within the farm's territory. Led by Catnip the Stray Cat. Its members are freelance contractors, not ideologues, loyal only to power and payment.

**Key Operatives:**

- **Bonbo the Rat** (老阴比鼠 — "The Sneaky Bastard Rat"): Senior henchman. Cunning opportunist. The brains of low-level operations. Highly likely to betray Catnip for a better offer.
- **Grumbles the Mouse** (水泥頭頭咕 — "Cement-Collar Grum"): Senior henchman. Mob enforcer. Loves to talk about "putting rats on ice." Professional pride. Less treacherous than Bonbo but just as self-serving.
- **Squeakers** (好或 — "Cunning Squeaky"): Crafty, talented, purely evil. Reliable foot soldier for the most malicious plots.
- **Ratticus** (天 — "Devourer of the Heavens, the Destroyer"): Hulking enforcer. Not bright, but immensely powerful. Loyal to the strongest leader.
- **Beelzebub** (巨霸鼠 — "The Colossal Tyrant Rat"): Hulking general. All muscle and menace.

**Joint Designations:**

- When Bonbo and Grumbles work together: 鼠鼠双煞 ("The Rodent Duo of Doom")
- When Ratticus and Beelzebub operate together: 吞天地 ("Devourers of Heaven and Earth")

### The Northern Horde

A nomadic, highly destructive force. A vast army of ravenous Mongolian rats that devour everything in their path. They are a force of nature, not a criminal organization. Led by Ratticus and Beelzebub. Temporarily co-opted by the Catnip Syndicate but represent a rival power bloc.

### The Dairy Mafia

A specialized criminal enterprise focused on high-stakes heists of dairy products. Led by Nutters the Squirrel. Mischievous but surprisingly effective. Direct competitors to the Catnip Syndicate.

**Key Operatives:**

- **Kuazihua (Fast Claw):** The acrobat. Speed legendary. Agility breathtaking. Spatial awareness abysmal. Executes a perfect triple-backflip off the weathervane only to land in a bucket of whitewash.
- **Tuntun (Hoardy McHoardface):** The quartermaster. Bottomless cheek pouches. No curation. A heist might net cheddar, acorns, a washer, a dried bean, and a confused ladybug.
- **Sha Lizi (Dumb Chestnut):** The muscle. Strong enough to roll walnuts twice his weight. Critical thinking skills of a sun-warmed rock. Pure heart. Mostly static.

### The Tango Trio

Three identical, dapper mice in crisp white linen suits. Not criminals. Not syndicate. They are the farm's Accidental Atmosphere Engineers. They hear the world's rhythm and dance to it.

- **Tito Tango:** The spokesman. Declares the music.
- **Paco Cha-Cha:** Hears lovesick ghosts and passionate cries.
- **Carlos Conga:** Plays the fence like a drum. Mid-backflip, lands in things. Declares them "rhythmic ointment."

Their canes are hollow and filled with different materials—rice, sand, ball bearings—allowing each mouse to produce a unique percussive sound.

## Why They Matter

The rodents are the farm's complexity. Where the core residents represent belonging, the rodents represent the question of belonging. Will they accept the farm? Will the farm accept them? Can they believe they are loved?

Catnip is the deepest of these questions. He has spent his whole life learning not to trust. And now, slowly, over the course of many stories, he is learning to trust. He is learning that the door was always open.

Natters is the comic version. He wants to be a warlord. He wants to control the dairy underground. He is learning—slowly—that ambition without wisdom is just a series of failed heists and crying squirrels.

The Three Blind Mice are the joyful version. They do not seek power. They do not seek control. They seek the beat. And in seeking the beat, they remind everyone that life is not a problem to be solved. It is a rhythm to be caught.

The rodents are the farm's edge. And the edges are where the story happens.
