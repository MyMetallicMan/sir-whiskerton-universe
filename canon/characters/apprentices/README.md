# The Apprentices

*The next generation. The ones who are learning.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the youngest members of the farm's feline family—the apprentices who learn from Sir Whiskerton, and who will one day inherit the farm's quiet stewardship.

They are the future. They are also the source of much of the farm's comedy, because learning is rarely graceful.

## Why They Matter

Ditto and Echo are the apprentices. They follow Sir Whiskerton everywhere. They repeat what he says. They cause chaos. They learn.

But their role is deeper than comedy. They are the *future*. They are the ones who will one day take up the quiet work of the farm—the witnessing, the holding, the presence. Sir Whiskerton is teaching them, whether they know it or not.

And they are teaching him. Ditto's echoing has taught Sir Whiskerton the value of the pause. Echo's drama has taught him that some truths are best spoken sideways.

The apprentices are not just students. They are *partners* in the ongoing work of the farm.

---

## The Balanced Apprenticeship

Ditto and Echo are intentionally balanced. Ditto is male, Echo is female. Ditto echoes, Echo deepens. Ditto finds his voice, Echo finds her expression. Each has their own arc, their own journey, their own way of belonging.

Their partnership is one of the quiet treasures of the universe.
