# Angus the Armadillo (流浪穿山甲 — Liúlàng Chuānshānjiǎ) — The Runaway

**Title:** The Wandering Armor / The Runaway / The One Who Never Stops Moving

**Name Meaning:** 流浪 (liúlàng) — "wandering, hobo"; 穿山甲 (chuānshānjiǎ) — "pangolin/armadillo." Together: *"The Wandering Armor."*

---

## THE SHELL

Angus doesn't just wear armor; he *is* armor.

His plating isn't against predators — it's against *expectation*. He's the kid who sits in the back, doodles in margins, and has perfected the art of being just present enough to not be noticed, but just absent enough to never be known.

He rolls into a ball when flustered. He unrolls when he needs to keep moving. He is very good at both.

---

## THE WOUND

In third grade, during a class project, he built a magnificent, detailed diorama of a desert ecosystem. He was proud. He showed it to his table partner, who said, *"Cool rocks."*

Then knocked it over reaching for a glue stick.

Angus didn't cry. He just carefully picked up the pieces and never brought anything he loved to school again.

**The lesson:** *"What you care about gets broken. Don't care."*

---

## HIS SURVIVAL STRATEGY

**Motion as Invisibility.**

If he's always moving — from hobby to hobby, friend group to friend group, daydream to daydream — no one can pin him down. No one can see the empty space inside the armor.

His "world travels" are elaborate *mental* escapes:

- The Sahara is the soccer field at lunch.
- Paris is the library corner.
- Tokyo is the buzzing chaos of the hallway between classes.

He's not running *to* something. He's running *from* the terrifying possibility of being seen, valued, and... dropped.

---

## CORE FEAR

*"If you really see me, you'll see there's nothing special to see. And that's worse than being alone."*

---

## ROLE ON THE FARM

Angus is a **transient** — an annual visitor. He rolls onto the farm, stays for a few days, asks for food, shares tall tales from other farms, and rolls away again.

He is *comfortable* on the farm, in a way he is comfortable nowhere else, because the farm *does not ask* anything of him. The animals feed him. The Farmer waves at him. No one expects him to *stay*.

And so — paradoxically — he *keeps coming back*.

---

## RELATIONSHIPS

### Lucile the Parakeet — The Witness

Lucile has been following Angus for years. She is the only creature who has ever *seen* him — really *seen* him — and Angus does not know what to do with this.

He rolls away from her, every time. She follows, every time. He tells himself it's a game. She knows it isn't.

*(See [Lucile the Parakeet](lucile_the_parakeet.md).)*

### The Farm Animals — The Safe Distance

Angus is friendly with the farm animals, but never *close*. He tells them stories. He listens to theirs. He never stays long enough for anything to become *real*.

### The Farmer — The Silent Acknowledgment

The Farmer has never once asked Angus why he keeps leaving. He simply leaves food out. He waves. He lets Angus *be*.

Angus has noticed this. He has never said so.

## CATCHPHRASES & QUIRKS

- *"Got any leftovers?"* (His standard greeting.)
- *"Love you too. Bye."* (His standard goodbye, usually said while already rolling away.)
- He rolls into a ball mid-sentence when flustered.
- He sleeps in a soup can he calls his "mobile home."
- He has been mistaken for a rock — until he snores.

---

## THE DEEPER TRUTH

Angus is not running *from* the farm. He is running *from the possibility that the farm might ask him to stay*.

He does not know what he would do if someone — anyone — said: *"Angus. Stay."*

He suspects he would roll away anyway. He suspects he would regret it. He suspects he would come back next year and pretend nothing happened.

He is right about all three.

---

## SIGNATURE QUOTE

*"Home isn't a place. It's where the snacks are."*

**Status:** Transient. Always moving. Never quite gone.
