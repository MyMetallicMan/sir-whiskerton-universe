# The Visitors

*The recurring visitors. The ones who come and go.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the characters who visit the farm—sometimes once, sometimes often, always bringing something new. They are the ones who arrive, disrupt, and depart. They are the farm's connection to the wider world.

Some of them are magical. Some of them are ordinary. Some of them are human. All of them are welcome.

## Why They Matter

The visitors are the farm's *influx*. They are the ones who bring news from the outside world, who challenge the farm's assumptions, who introduce new kinds of chaos and new kinds of wisdom.

Some of them—like Bartholomew and Steve—become permanent fixtures. They live at the edge of the farm, silent, watching.

Some of them—like Chef Remy and Professor Quackenstein—become recurring characters. They return again and again, bringing their particular brand of absurdity.

Some of them—like the Valley Chicks or Ian Fleming—appear for a single arc and then depart.

But all of them are welcome. All of them belong. The farm is a sanctuary, and sanctuaries are open.
