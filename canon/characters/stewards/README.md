# The Stewards

*The founding characters. The heart of the farm.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the three characters at the center of the Sir Whiskerton Universe. They are the ones who hold the farm together—not through authority, but through presence.

The animals call them "the Stewards." They are the keepers of the sanctuary.

## Why They Matter

The farm has many characters. But these three are the foundation. Sir Whiskerton is the steward of the farm's peace. The Farmer is the axis around which the animals orbit. Martha is the quiet love that holds them all.

Every story, in some way, touches one of these three. Every story, in some way, returns to them.

They are not heroes. They are not rulers. They are simply *present*. And that is enough.
