# Main Characters

*The canonical roster of the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

**IMPORTANT:** Please remember that this universe is a **safe place** — a place of peace, understanding, and belonging. Even the craziest characters are accepted for their unique quirks, and this message must always be present.

**The farm is a crazy but welcoming place.**

---

## How to Use This Document

This is the *roster* — the cast list. It is not the *bible*. Full character files exist separately, in the folders that reflect their role on the farm.

This document serves as:

- A quick reference for anyone writing a story.
- A cross-index for anyone tracing relationships.
- A single page where the *main* characters are always present, even if their full files are scattered across the repo.

Every character listed here has, or will have, a full file. When you see a link, follow it. When you see a title without a link, the file is still being written.

## The Stewards

### 0. The Farmer

**Chinese Name:** 糊涂农夫 (Hútú Nóngfū) — "Scatterbrained Farmer"

**Role:** Human owner of the farm; involved in strange, seemingly meaningless activities.

**Personality:** Odd, quirky, and somewhat detached from the animals. He talks to *everything* — not just scarecrows, but melons, piñatas, a lost kite, trees, and the wind. His shyness is the core of him: he is a man who *needs* to talk, but who is too shy to talk to the one person who would truly understand him — Martha.

**Key Relationships:**

- **Martha** — The one person he cannot bring himself to speak to plainly. The tension of their relationship is the gentlest, most heartwarming subplot on the farm.
- **Sir Whiskerton** — His most consistent silent companion. The Farmer talks *through* Whiskerton as much as *to* him.
- **The Scarecrows** — He speaks to all of them, but *Steve* is the most important, because Steve is closest to the barn and the coop.

**Full File:** [humans/the_farmer.md](humans/the_farmer.md)

### Martha

**Role:** The owner of the neighboring farm; the Farmer's quiet counterpart and the story's warm, beating heart.

**Personality:** Sweet, wholesome, patient. She sees the good, quirky heart beneath the Farmer's bashful exterior. She finds his oddities endearing rather than strange.

**Key Relationships:**

- **The Farmer** — Their relationship is a pure, unforced, gentle romance. The biggest obstacle isn't drama or conflict; it is simply two kind, shy people trying to pluck up the courage to share a piece of pie.

**Full File:** [humans/martha.md](humans/martha.md)

## The Main Character

### 1. Sir Whiskerton

**Chinese Name:** 胡子爵士 (Húzi Juéshì)

**Why funny?** "胡子" (húzi) means "whiskers," and "爵士" (juéshì) is "knight." The name sounds regal but is undercut by the silly image of a cat with exaggerated whiskers. Bonus: the wordplay on "胡子" (whiskers) and "糊涂" (silly/confused) adds a layer of humor.

**Role:** Farm detective, philosopher, and self-proclaimed genius.

**Personality:** Clever, confident, slightly vain, but deeply caring for his friends.

**Latest Adventures:** Solving mysteries like cursed wheelbarrows, tie-dye cows, and philosophical debates with Bartholomew the piñata.

**Future Potential:**

- Face challenges to his authority (e.g., a rival detective).
- Confront personal vulnerabilities (e.g., fear of cucumbers).

**Full File:** [animals/sir_whiskerton.md](animals/sir_whiskerton.md)

## The Core Cast

### 2. Rufus the Dog ("Radioactive Doggie")

**Chinese Name:** 发光狗 (Fāguāng Gǒu)

**Why funny?** "发光" (fāguāng) means "glowing," referencing his green fur. The absurdity of a literal glowing dog is hilarious.

**Nickname:** "Radioactive Doggie" due to glowing green fur.

**Personality:** Energetic, clumsy, loyal, and eager to help.

**Latest Adventures:** Struggled with counting sheep, eventually finding creative solutions by naming them instead.

**Future Potential:**

- Discover hidden talents (e.g., tracking skills).
- Accidentally stumble into leadership roles.

### 3. Doris the Hen

**Chinese Name:** 八卦母鸡 (Bāguà Mǔjī)

**Why funny?** "八卦" (bāguà) means "gossipy," perfectly capturing Doris's dramatic personality. The term is commonly used in Chinese pop culture.

**Role:** Leader of the hens and chief gossipmonger.

**Personality:** Dramatic, easily flustered, and prone to panicking.

**Latest Adventures:** Dealt with the Great Feed Fiasco and clashed with geese over mixed-up feed.

**Future Potential:**

- Start a farm newspaper or gossip column.
- Solve a mystery involving missing eggs.

### 4. Harriet and Lillian (Doris's Entourage)

**Role:** Supporting hens who follow Doris everywhere.

**Harriet** — 八卦快嘴鸡 (Bāguà Kuàizuǐ Jī) — "Gossip Fast-Mouth Hen." Slightly more level-headed than Doris. Signature line: *"Farm headline: Sir Whiskerton is bald! ...What? Just shedding? Uh... details don't matter!"*

**Lillian** — 晕倒戏精鸡 (Yūndǎo Xìjīng Jī) — "Fainting Drama Queen Hen." Prone to fainting at the slightest provocation. Signature line: *"Oh no! A dandelion! It's... it's... too terrifying!"* [faints]

**Future Potential:**

- Develop individual personalities and subplots.
- Become amateur sleuths alongside Sir Whiskerton.

### 5. Porkchop the Pig

**Chinese Name:** 猪大排 (Zhū Dàpái)

**Why funny?** "大排" (dàpái) means "pork chop," but it's also slang for a "big shot," mocking Porkchop's sarcastic ego.

**Role:** Food-loving pig with a sarcastic streak.

**Personality:** Laid-back, good-natured, and witty.

**Latest Adventures:** Helped investigate the cursed wheelbarrow; considered getting a tattoo after meeting Lester.

**Future Potential:**

- Discover hidden artistic talents (e.g., painting or gardening).
- Attempt weight loss or compete in farmyard contests.

### 6. Ferdinand the Duck

**Chinese Name:** 歌剧鸭 (Gējù Yā)

**Why funny?** "歌剧" (gējù) means "opera," highlighting his delusional singing talent. The contrast between a duck and opera is absurd.

**Role:** Self-proclaimed "singing sensation." Fortune teller.

**Personality:** Dramatic, self-absorbed, and always seeking admiration.

**Latest Adventures:** Lost his voice temporarily, clashed with a peacock, fell in love with Catnip's girlfriend.

**Future Potential:**

- Form a band or start a rivalry with another musical talent.
- Learn humility through teamwork.

### 7. Bingo the Dog

**Chinese Name:** 嗥哥 (Háo Gē) — "Howl Bro"

**Why funny?** "嗥" (háo) means "to howl," directly referencing his talent for dramatic, soulful howling. "哥" (gē) means "bro," giving him a laid-back, cool-dog vibe.

**Role:** Another farm dog, known for napping and howling.

**Personality:** Quiet, unassuming, but talented at howling.

**Latest Adventures:** Joined Ferdinand in a disastrous duet combining quacks and howls.

**Future Potential:**

- Train for a farmyard competition.
- Take on unexpected leadership roles.

### 8. Jazzpurr the Beatnik Cat

**Chinese Name:** 爵士猫 (Juéshì Māo)

**Why funny?** "爵士" (juéshì) means "jazz," but it's also a pun on "爵士" (knight), making him a "jazz knight."

**Role:** A beatnik cat who arrived on the farm with Captain Meowtronic.

**Personality:** Eccentric, poetic, and always grooving to his own rhythm.

**Dress:** Wears a black beret.

**Latest Adventures:** Brought chaos and creativity to the farm, hosting poetry readings and bongo sessions.

**Future Potential:**

- Start a poetry club or host open mic nights.

### 9. Ditto the Echoing Kitten

**Chinese Name:** 复读猫 (Fùdú Māo)

**Why funny?** "复读" (fùdú) means "to repeat," mimicking Ditto's echoing habit. It's also a playful jab at students who "repeat" lessons without understanding.

**Role:** A young kitten who imitates everything Sir Whiskerton says and does. Sir Whiskerton is his mentor.

**Personality:** Playful, eager to learn, and a bit of a nuisance, but with a heart of gold.

**Latest Adventures:** Became Sir Whiskerton's shadow, learning the ropes of farm life and occasionally driving everyone crazy with his echoing habit.

**Future Potential:**

- Find his own voice and stop echoing others.

### 10. Count Catula

**Chinese Name:** 吸血鬼猫 (Xīxuèguǐ Māo)

**Why funny?** "吸血鬼" (xīxuèguǐ) means "vampire," but pairing it with a cat in a cape is ridiculous.

**Role:** A self-proclaimed vampire cat who arrived on the farm with a flair for the dramatic.

**Personality:** Theatrical, mysterious, and a bit ridiculous.

**Latest Adventures:** Stirred up chaos with his dramatic antics, teamed up with Catnip, but eventually became the farm's "Lord of Nighttime Naps."

### 11. Bessie the Tie-Dye Cow

**Chinese Name:** 彩虹牛 (Cǎihóng Niú)

**Why funny?** "彩虹" (cǎihóng) means "rainbow," emphasizing her hippie vibe. Cows aren't rainbow-colored, making it whimsical.

**Role:** A cow who became a hippie after a paint-related transformation. She wears love beads, a mood ring, and rose-tinted glasses.

**Personality:** Laid-back, dreamy, and full of "peace and love" vibes. Her favorite song is "Tip Toe Through the Tulips" by Tiny Tim.

**Dialog:** Uses ample slang from the 1960s, such as "groovy," "make love not war," and "I'm not the fuzz, man, chill out."

**Latest Adventures:** Accidentally turned herself into a tie-dye masterpiece, embraced her inner hippie, and became the farm's unofficial therapist.

### 12. Lucifer the Chipmunk

**Chinese Name:** 戏精胖仙·路西法 (Xìjīng Pàng Xiān · Lùxīfǎ) — "Drama Queen Chubby Immortal · Lucifer"

**Nickname:** 红魔叨叨鼠 (Hóngmó Dāodāo Shǔ) — "Red Devil Chattermouse"

**Role:** A chubby chipmunk with a flair for the dramatic.

**Personality:** Over-the-top, self-absorbed, and always seeking attention.

**Catchphrase:** *"This immortal decrees: YOU MUST LET ME STAND ON YOUR SHOULDER!"*

**Latest Adventures:** Declared himself "anointed" after being covered in red paint, later relapsed into his red paint obsession, and inspired Bessie's tie-dye transformation.

**Position:** Always tries to get on the shoulder of an animal and whisper to it about the joys of a progressive lifestyle.

### 13. Chef Remy LeRaccoon

**Chinese Name:** 疯狂厨师 (Fēngkuáng Chúshī)

**Why funny?** "疯狂" (fēngkuáng) means "crazy," fitting his mad-scientist cooking.

**Role:** A "mad scientist" raccoon who operates a strange gourmet laboratory.

**Latest Adventures:** Experiments with glow-in-the-dark pickles, invisible feed, and time-travel feed.

**Sidekick / Assistant:** **Doctor Notorious** — a white lab rat with a determined, matter-of-fact attitude.

### 14. Gnomeo the Garden Gnome

**Chinese Name:** 屁果仙 (Pì Guǒ Xiān) — "Fart Fruit Fairy"

**Why funny?** "屁果" (pì guǒ) — "Fart Fruit." "仙" (xiān) — "Immortal" or "Magical Being." The combination is absurd and delightful.

**Role:** A wandering gnome who likes to play tricks on the animals of the farm.

**Personality:** Mischievous and playful.

**Latest Adventures:** Moves and speaks as a lawn ornament.

### 15. Molly Quackers

**Chinese Name:** 魔音鸭·夸克莉丝 (Mó Yīn Yā · Kuā Kè Lì Sī) — "The Enchanting Diva-Duck · Quacklise"

**Why funny?** "魔音" (mó yīn) is often used to describe ear-piercingly bad singing, but Molly thinks she's a superstar. "夸克" (kuā kè) also means "quark" in physics, making her sound like a scientific-artistic anomaly.

**Bonus Nickname:** 鸭斯卡歌后 (Yā Sī Kā Gē Hòu) — "The Duck-scar (Oscar) Song Queen."

**Role:** Artiste extraordinaire and singing sensation.

**Personality:** Creative and captivating.

**Latest Adventures:** Painted murals, composed operatic quacks, and hosted poetry readings under the moonlight.

### 16. Mr. Ducky

**Chinese Name:** 忽悠鸭 (Hūyou Yā) — "The Hustle Duck"

**Why funny?** "忽悠" (hūyou) is slang for "scam" or "hustle," implying he's a fast-talking con artist — but in a silly, non-malicious way.

**Role:** A sales-duck. He is not necessarily bad, just greedy and underhanded. He is always trying to sell things to make money and gain influence. His ideas and plans are often absurd and outlandish. Most farm animals sigh and roll their eyes when he comes to the farm.

**Personality:** High-pressure, capitalist merchant.

**Notes:** He has had numerous race cockroaches; a Mr. Golden that disappeared right before a race, and Mr. Pickleworth who is simply a shriveled pickle.

**Alternative Nicknames (Used by Other Characters):**

- 奸商鸭 (Jiānshāng Yā) — "Profiteer Duck" (Doris's sarcastic nickname)
- 鸭骗子 (Yā Piànzi) — "Duck Scammer" (Porkchop's grumble when ripped off)
- 鸭总 (Yā Zǒng) — "Duck CEO" (his self-given title on business cards)
