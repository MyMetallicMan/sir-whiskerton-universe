# The Sanctuary Covenant

*A welcome note. A promise. A gentle word for anyone who enters the farm.*

---

## To Anyone Who Reads These Stories

Welcome.

You have found your way to a place that exists between the lines of maps and the ticks of clocks. This is not a manual of rules, but a map of the heart.

The farm doesn't run on schedules or spreadsheets; it runs on a quiet, persistent magic woven from purrs, creaks, and the courage to try again.

If you are new here: **come in.**

If you have been here for years: **your chair is still waiting.**

---

## The Promise

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is a **sanctuary**.

Its promise is simple: *the feeling of belonging you find here will remain constant, no matter how many new friends join us at the table.*

Whatever changes, wherever the stories grow, the farm will still be:

- A place where oddity is dignity.
- A place where no one is asked to earn their welcome.
- A place where problems are solved with patience, not force.
- A place where the small things — a button, a purr, a shared silence — are as important as the large.

---

## The Test

If you ever wonder whether a new story, a new character, or a new addition truly belongs on the farm, ask this:

> *"If I walked down the dirt road, would Rufus bound up with the same uncomplicated joy? Would Martha wave with the same unconditional care? Would Sir Whiskerton's presence be the same relief?"*

If the answer is yes, it belongs. If the answer is no, something has drifted.

*(See [The Visitor's Test](../writing-guidelines/the_visitors_test.md).)*

---

## The Benediction

The story never ends. It simply rests in your hands, ready for the next note.

**Jingle on.**
