# The Divine Llama

**仙踪驼 (Xiān Zōng Tuó)** — "The Immortal of Hidden Paths"

**Title:** Keeper of Stillness • Oracle of the Unhurried • Master of the Subtle Spit

## Origins: Not Arrived—Unfolded

No one knows when the Divine Llama appeared on the farm.

She did not walk in.

She did not teleport.

She did not arrive via malfunctioning elevator or rogue hot-air balloon.

She was simply… there—one morning, beneath the old oak, chewing thoughtfully on a sprig of lavender, as if she had been waiting since the first blade of grass pushed through the soil.

"She didn't come to the farm," Bessie once mused, mood ring pulsing soft gold. "The farm grew still enough to notice her."

Some say she emerged from the mist after Zephyr meditated too hard.

Others claim she stepped out of the Cloud Library's quietest shelf.

Ditto insists she arrived in a package labeled "FRAGILE: WISDOM (SHAKE GENTLY)."

Porkchop says she's just really good at standing still.

Only Sir Whiskerton, after a long silence and a shared cup of tea, offered the closest truth:

"She's not from anywhere. She's from nowhere important—and that's why she's so hard to ignore."

## Nature & Practice

The Divine Llama does not speak—not in words, at least. Her communication is layered:

- A slow blink = "Yes."
- Two slow blinks = "Yes… and you already knew that."
- A soft snort = "Try again."
- A gentle spit (rare, precise, never aggressive) = "This moment matters. Pay attention."

*(Note: Spit is always aimed at objects—not beings. Once, it landed on Genghis's gold chain, causing it to rust mid-monologue. No one complained.)*

She offers no advice. No mantras. No five-step plans to enlightenment.

Instead, she holds space.

Animals come to her sun-porch not to be fixed—but to remember how to be:

- Lucifer sits in silence for 17 minutes, then whispers, "What if… I just… didn't?"
- Doris arrives mid-panic, leaves mid-sigh, muttering, "Oh. Oh, okay."
- Sir Whiskerton brings his tea, says nothing, and departs with his tail held just a little less tightly.

Her only "teaching" is a small stone near her perch, etched with two characters:

**止觀 (Zhǐ Guān)**

"Stop. Observe."

## Powers & Limitations

| Gifts | Boundaries |
|-------|------------|
| Calms storms (internal and meteorological) | Will not intervene in snack theft |
| Turns arguments into shared silence | Cannot stop Ditto from echoing |
| Causes lavender to bloom out of season | Refuses to judge poetry contests |
| Her presence lowers ambient chaos by 73%* | Will not explain why |

*Figure provided by Chef Remy after "scientific observation" (he napped near her for 3 hours and woke up "weirdly at peace").*

## Canonical Moments

- **Col 16-Book 288:** When Bartholomew the Piñata suffered an existential crisis over "the meaning of candy in a meaningless world," the Llama simply stood beside him—until he chose to open, scattering joy not from obligation, but grace.

- **Col 17-Book 312:** During The Great Moo-ditation Schism, she ended the tie-dye vs. monochrome cow feud by lying down in the middle of the pasture. Both sides gathered around her—and fell asleep in a spiral. Peace lasted 4 hours, 12 minutes.

- **Col 19-Book 334:** When Tony the Bear and the Yodeling Fish battled for a love interest (a very confused alpaca), the Llama stared at the alpaca until it calmly walked away—toward a field of clover—and everyone realized: Sometimes, the best choice is no choice at all.
