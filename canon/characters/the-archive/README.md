# The Archive

*The retired characters. The one-off appearances. The ones who have moved on.*

---

## Purpose

This folder is the archive of characters who have appeared in the Sir Whiskerton Universe but who are no longer active—either because they retired, because they appeared only once, or because they have moved on to other things.

They are not forgotten. They are *remembered*. They are the farm's collective memory of everyone who has ever visited.

## Why They Matter

Not every character can stay. Some arrive, teach a lesson, and depart. Some are part of the farm's history and are remembered but not seen. Some simply no longer fit the direction of the universe.

But none of them are forgotten. The archive is a *memory*. It honors the ones who came before.

And sometimes—sometimes—a retired character returns. They walk back onto the farm, older, wiser, and just as welcome as ever.

The door is always open. The archive is not a locked room.
