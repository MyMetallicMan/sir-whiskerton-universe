# Animals of the Sir Whiskerton Universe

*The cats, dogs, pigs, hens, geese, ducks, dragons, and assorted creatures who live on, under, above, and around the farm.*

---

## About

The Sir Whiskerton universe is, at its heart, an animal story. The farm belongs to them. The humans are the guests. Every animal here—from the smallest nervous chipmunk to the largest dragon who believes he is a cat—has a role, a personality, and a place in the world.

This folder collects every animal character who appears in canon. Some are permanent residents. Some are recurring visitors. Some pass through once, leave a mark, and move on. All of them, in their own way, are the farm.

## How to Use This Folder

**For writers.** Each animal bio contains their role, personality, appearance, relationships, and story potential. Cross-reference with the human bios when an animal and a human share a scene—the interaction dynamics are usually noted in both files.

**For readers.** These bios are written to be read in any order. They are not a timeline; they are a roster.

**For contributors.** If you're adding a new animal character, follow the format of the existing files and add an entry to this README in the appropriate category.

## A Note on Tone

The animals of the Sir Whiskerton universe are, without exception, characters in their own right. They are not props. They are not allegories. They are *people*—with quirks, flaws, hopes, and fears. Even the ones who cause problems (Bandit, Mr. Ducky) are never cruel. This is a soft universe. The animals reflect that.

When writing them, remember:

- **They have secret lives the humans do not know about.** This is the source of much of the comedy and all of the warmth.
- **They are not fools.** They are fully realized beings with their own agendas, friendships, and philosophies.
- **They are loved.** Even the peripheral characters matter. Even the one-off guests leave a mark.
