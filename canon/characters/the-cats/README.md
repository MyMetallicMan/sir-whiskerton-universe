# The Cats

*The feline society. The ones who live on the edges.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the feline characters who are not part of the farm's core residents. They are the cats of the neighboring farms, the cats who wander in from elsewhere, the cats who occupy the edges of the farm's ecosystem.

They are rivals, allies, philosophers, and jesters. They are the farm's mirror, its complication, and its delight.

## Why They Matter

The cats are the farm's complexity. Where the core residents represent belonging, the cats represent the *question* of belonging. Will they accept the farm? Will the farm accept them? Can they believe they are loved?

Catnip is the deepest of these questions. He has spent his whole life learning not to trust. And now, slowly, over the course of many stories, he is learning to trust. He is learning that the door was always open.

Genghis is the *comic* version of the same question. He wants to be important. He wants to be respected. He is learning—slowly—that respect is granted, not taken.

Bigcat is the *external* version. He wants to control. He wants to acquire. He is learning that control is not belonging.

The cats are the farm's edge. And the edges are where the story happens.
