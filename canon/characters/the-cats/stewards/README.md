# The Stewards

*The center of the farm. The one who stays. The one who is learning to stay.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the two cats at the center of the feline society — and at the center of the entire Sir Whiskerton Universe.

Sir Whiskerton is the farm's steward. Ditto is his apprentice. Together, they form the emotional core of the farm.

## Why They Matter

Every cat in the Sir Whiskerton Universe is asking the same question: *"Do I belong?"*

Sir Whiskerton is the one who answers. Not with words. With presence. He stays. He witnesses. He holds the farm's center so that everyone else can orbit around it.

Ditto is the one who is *learning* to answer. He begins as an echo—repeating everything he hears. But his journey is toward something deeper. He is learning to find his own voice. To speak his own truth. To become, in his own way, a steward of the farm.

They are the heart of the feline society. Everything else flows from them.
