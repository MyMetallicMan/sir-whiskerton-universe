# The Feline Forces

*A strategic overview of the farm's cat population.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the *strategic overview* of the feline society — a document meant for anyone who needs a quick, high-level understanding of every cat on the farm, their alliances, their threat levels, and their narrative function.

It is a reference document, not a story. It is what Sir Whiskerton would compile if he had to brief a new arrival on the farm's political landscape.

## Why This Exists

The cats are a complex society. There are stewards. There are sovereign exiles. There are antagonists and artists and powers and new arrivals. Keeping track of all of them — and their relationships, their alliances, their rivalries — is not always easy.

This folder exists to make it easy.

It is the farm's intelligence report. It is Sir Whiskerton's mental dossier, written down.
