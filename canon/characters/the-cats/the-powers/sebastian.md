# Sebastian

*The Wicked-Claw Gentleman, the Bowler-Hatted Enigma, and the Cat Who Controls the Farmer. The Mystery.*

**Chinese Name:** 邪爪绅士 (Xié Zhuǎ Shēnshì) — "Wicked-Claw Gentleman"

---

## One-Line Essence

Sebastian is the farm's mystery. He is the cat who exists at the edge of what the farm understands. He is not a villain. He is not a hero. He is a question that has never been answered — and may never need to be.

He is the farm's proof that some things are not meant to be solved.

---

## Appearance

A sleek, well-fed tomcat with short, dense fur the color of coal ash. His most striking features are twofold: first, a pair of extra claws on each front paw — subtle, but unmistakable once you notice them. Second, a bowler hat, brushed to a muted sheen, perched at a permanent angle. No one has ever seen him without it.

His eyes are a pale, sharp green — the color of unripe apples. He moves with a slow, deliberate presence, each step placed with precision.

He carries an air of quiet authority. He does not demand attention. He *receives* it.

---

## Core Philosophy

**The Doctrine of the Unknown.**

Sebastian believes that mystery is not a problem to be solved — it is a condition to be inhabited. He does not explain himself. He does not correct rumors. He does not clarify.

He knows that the farm does not need to understand him. It only needs to *notice* him. And noticing him is enough.

His unspoken motto: *"You do not need to know me. You only need to wonder."*

---

## Role on the Farm

He is the farm's **Quiet Force.** He is not a leader. He is not a follower. He is the one who exists in the spaces between — the one who watches, and waits, and occasionally acts in ways that no one fully understands.

He exerts a subtle influence over the Farmer. No one knows how. No one knows why. The Farmer simply responds to Sebastian's presence — opening doors, setting out saucers of cream, gently brushing his fur — as if by instinct, not by command.

The other cats observe this with a mix of unease and awe. Sir Whiskerton, ever the diplomat, treats Sebastian with a careful, watchful respect. He does not try to figure Sebastian out. He simply observes, and waits, and is ready if the mystery ever chooses to reveal itself.

---

## Personality

- **Dismissive.** He does not waste words. He does not waste glances. He does not waste time. If he speaks, it is because the moment demands it. If he moves, it is because the movement matters.
- **Ageless.** He appears to be a mature adult cat — but there is something in his bearing, his stillness, his patience, that suggests he has been alive far longer than his body should allow. Some believe he is hundreds of years old. Sebastian has never corrected this.
- **Quietly Menacing.** He is not threatening. But there is a *weight* to him. The other animals feel it. Even the dogs lower their voices when he is near.
- **Deeply Amused.** Beneath the enigma, there is a flicker of something dry and knowing. He enjoys the farm's chaos. He enjoys watching the young cats scramble. He enjoys being — for want of a better word — *Sebastian.*

He does not explain any of this. He does not need to.

---

## The Origin

No one knows where Sebastian came from. No one knows how long he has been alive. No one knows why he chose this farm, or what he does when the animals are asleep.

There are theories. Some say he was once a pampered show cat who escaped from a Victorian manor. Some say he is the last survivor of an ancient line of mousers who served royalty. Some say he is simply a very old, very clever cat who decided one day to live on a farm and never explained himself.

Sebastian has never confirmed or denied any of this. He simply *is.*

---

## Key Relationships

### Sir Whiskerton — The Respectful Distance

Sir Whiskerton treats Sebastian with careful, watchful respect. He does not try to figure him out. He does not try to control him. He simply observes — and is ready.

Sebastian, for his part, seems to approve of Sir Whiskerton. The two of them can often be found on opposite ends of the barn roof, in silent mutual acknowledgment. They do not speak. They do not need to. They are both cats who prefer to watch.

### The Farmer — The Puppet and the Master

Sebastian controls the Farmer. No one knows how. The Farmer himself does not seem to realize he is being controlled. He simply — responds. He opens doors. He sets out cream. He brushes fur. He gazes at Sebastian with a kind of quiet contentment.

The other animals find this unnerving. Sebastian finds it *fitting.*

### Count Catula — The Fellow Mysterious

Sebastian and Count Catula have an understanding. They are both performing. They are both watching. They both know the other is not what they seem. But where Catula performs *loudly,* Sebastian performs *silently.*

They do not speak of this. They do not need to.

### Genghis — The Unimpressed Audience

Genghis has tried, on multiple occasions, to recruit Sebastian into his schemes. Sebastian has never said no. He has never said yes. He has simply *looked* at Genghis — a long, slow, unimpressed look — until Genghis backed away, embarrassed, and pretended the conversation had never happened.

## Catchphrases & Quirks

- *(A long, slow blink.)*
- *(An expression that is neither approval nor disapproval.)*
- *"人类以为他们在养猫...呵呵。"* — *"Humans think they own cats... Heh."*
- *(Silence. Continued silence. A whisker twitch.)*

He wears a bowler hat. He has extra claws. He controls the Farmer. He never explains himself. He disappears for long periods of time and reappears as if he had never left.

He is, in every sense of the word, *Sebastian.*

---

## The Deeper Truth

Sebastian is afraid of being known.

This is the deepest truth of his character. He has spent his entire — very long — life cultivating mystery because mystery is *protection.* If no one knows him, no one can hurt him. If no one understands him, no one can expect anything from him. If no one sees him, no one can reject him.

He is not evil. He is not even mysterious by nature. He is mysterious by *choice.* And that choice was made a very, very long time ago.

He is the farm's quietest wound. And he has no intention of ever showing it.

---

## Why He Belongs

Sebastian is the farm's necessary reminder that **not everyone needs to be understood to be accepted.** He is the one who lives at the edge, the one who does not quite fit, the one who is welcome anyway.

He is the farm's proof that belonging is not about being known. It is about being *given space.* The farm does not demand that Sebastian explain himself. It simply makes room for him. And Sebastian, in his own quiet way, has made room for the farm.

It is not a friendship. It is not even a relationship. It is a *coexistence.* And it is enough.

## Status

Active. Unknowable. In the barn, on the porch, on the roof — wherever he chooses to be. Watching. Waiting.

He is the farm's mystery. And the farm is content to let the mystery remain.
