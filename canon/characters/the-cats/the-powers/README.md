## Purpose

This folder contains the cats who are not central to the farm's daily life, but who exist at its edges — the ones who provide depth, mystery, and the occasional lesson. They are the farm's **forces**.

## Why They Matter

These cats are not the farm's center. But they are the farm's **depth**. They are the reminder that the universe is larger than the barnyard, and that not everything can be explained.

**Sebastian** is the mystery. He seems ageless. He wears a bowler hat. He has extra claws. He exerts a subtle control over the Farmer. His true origins and motives remain unknown.

**Felinara** is the divine. She is the Guardian of Cat Heaven — a supreme being who judges worthy feline souls. But her divine nature is undercut by very cat-like habits, like mid-prophecy paw-licking and napping on a sunbeam throne.

**Bacchus** is the chaos. He is a free-spirited cat from a neighboring farm who loves catnip and rolling in grass. He is the one who will lead the farm animals on unsanctioned adventures.

**Sylvester** is the hidden warmth. He is a grumpy barn cat whose smiles are so potent they magically transform the environment, bringing instant joy. He is grumpy, but he has depths.

**Sir Cattenon** is the vanity made visible. He is Sir Whiskerton's pompous brother, constantly trying to outshine him, and constantly failing. He is not a villain. He is a tragedy wrapped in a cape.

**Mei Li** is the order. She is Sir Whiskerton's cousin and a visiting Etiquette Enforcer. She believes any problem can be solved with the correct application of protocol and a perfectly steeped cup of tea.

Together, they are the farm's *edges*. And the edges are where the story happens.
