# The Retinue

*Bigcat's crew. The ones who enforce the will of the gold chain.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains Bigcat and his retinue — the cats who live on the neighboring farm and who serve as the farm's externalized antithesis.

They are not villains. They are **forces**. They are the answer to the question: *"What would the farm become if it chose power over belonging?"*

## Why They Matter

Bigcat believes that power is the only true reality. Sentiment is a weakness. Anything not under his direct control is a flaw in the universal design. He sees Sir Whiskerton's farm not as a community, but as an "unincorporated asset" and its gentle chaos as "gross operational negligence."

His retinue mirrors and enforces his authority.

- **Catticus** is the brain. He reads the maps, scouts the fences, and identifies true points of weakness. He respects Sir Whiskerton as a worthy tactical opponent — a sentiment he keeps carefully hidden from his boss.
- **Putter** is the brawn. He is force incarnate. But he has a secret: he is deeply sensitive. He is afraid of butterflies. He loves yarn. He once defected for an afternoon because the Valley Chicks told him he had "pretty stripes."
- **Goliath** is the engineer. He builds the Rube Goldberg-esque contraptions for Bigcat's schemes. His machines always work exactly once, spectacularly, and then fail in a way that creates a bigger, messier problem than the one they were meant to solve.

Together, they are the storm cloud on the farm's horizon. A threat that is often more comical than critical. But whose existence reminds every creature just what they are choosing when they choose kindness, community, and a shared bowl under a forgiving sun.
