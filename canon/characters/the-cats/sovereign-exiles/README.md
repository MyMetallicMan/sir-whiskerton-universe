## Purpose

This folder contains the two cats who test the farm's love — the ones who cannot believe they belong, and who spend their lives trying to prove that they don't. They are the farm's mirrors. They ask the unspoken question.

## Why They Matter

Catnip and Genghis are two answers to the same question. *How do you live when you cannot believe you are loved?*

Catnip answers with schemes. He steals. He plots. He pushes the boundaries of the farm's patience — over and over — trying to find the limit where love ends. And every time, the farm answers: *"There is no limit. Stay anyway."*

Genghis answers with demands. He builds a kingdom out of straw. He wears a gold chain made of brass. He declares himself the "kingpin" — over and over — trying to prove that he matters. And every time, the farm answers: *"You already matter. You can stand down."*

They are not villains. They are the farm's **mirror**. They show the farm what fear looks like. They show the farm what love is for.
