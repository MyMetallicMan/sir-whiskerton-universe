# The Artists

*The ones who transform the farm through beauty. The soundtrack and the theatre.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the two cats who serve as the farm's artists — the ones who make the unseen visible, who sanctify the mundane, who give the farm's emotional life a shape.

They are not performers. They are **priests of beauty**.

## Why They Matter

Jazzpurr is the farm's *soundtrack*. He does not solve problems. He provides the music that makes the problems bearable. He operates The Subterranean Jazz Den — a sanctuary for souls who feel out of step with the sunlit world. He hosts "The Midnight Dig." He writes poetry about the way the light falls on the barn at dawn.

Count Catula is the farm's *theatre*. He does not perform for applause. He performs to make the dark less frightening — for himself as much as for anyone else. He curates moonlit reading hours for insomniac sheep. He hosts "The Dusk Club." His cape is armor. His fangs are a deflector. His vulnerability is the bravest thing on the farm.

Together, they are the farm's **soul made visible**. They are the reason the farm is not just a place to live — it is a place to *feel*.
