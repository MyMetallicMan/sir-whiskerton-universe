## Why They Matter

The modern voices are the farm's *evolution made audible*. They are the proof that the Sir Whiskerton Universe is not a fixed thing. It is a living thing. It is a place where new voices arrive, new sounds emerge, and new forms of beauty are discovered.

**MC Scratches** is the architect. He writes complex, cerebral rhymes with a tiny, beat-up notebook perpetually in his paw. He is serious, deliberate, and believes rap is a high art form. He is the thinker of the duo.

**Lil' Paws** is the heartbeat. He is pure, unadulterated energy — a hype man, beatboxer, and master of vibes. He doesn't write lyrics down; he feels them. He is the duo's connection to the crowd.

**DJ Fader Fuzz** is the sonic architect. He does not perform. He *scores*. He provides the soundtrack to the farm's life, sampling its rhythms and transforming them into something new. His music is the farm's heartbeat, made electronic.

Together, they are the farm's *modern voice*. They are the future.
