# MC Scratches

*The Lyrical Architect, the Perfectionist, and the Half of a Whole. The Modern Voice — Words.*

**Chinese Name:** 说唱猫·刮擦 (Shuōchàng Māo · Guācā) — "Rap Cat · Scratch"

---

## One-Line Essence

MC Scratches is the farm's lyrical architect. He is the brains of The Most Feline — the one who crafts the complex wordplay, the multisyllabic rhymes, the "meaningful bars." He is not the heart of the duo. He is the spine.

He is the farm's proof that hip-hop is not noise — it is poetry with a pulse.

---

## Appearance

A lean, sharp-featured cat with short, well-groomed fur the color of a vinyl record — matte black with a faint glossy sheen. His eyes are a deep, focused brown, and they narrow when he is thinking — which is most of the time.

He is always dressed with intention. A branded baseball jersey (his favorite reads "9-LIVES & LYCAN"), a perfectly fitted cap, and a single, not-too-flashy gold chain. He carries a tiny, beat-up notebook in one paw at all times, and a stub of pencil tucked behind one ear.

He does not dress to be seen. He dresses to be remembered.

---

## Core Philosophy

**The Doctrine of Precision.**

MC Scratches believes that words are not tools — they are weapons. They are instruments. They are the most powerful thing in the universe, and they must be wielded with precision.

He believes that rap is a high art form. He believes that every syllable must earn its place. He believes that a single misplaced rhyme is not a mistake — it is a betrayal.

His motto: *"If it doesn't land, it doesn't live."*

---

## Role on the Farm

He is the **lyricist and frontman** of **The Most Feline** — the farm's premier hip-hop duo. Alongside his partner Lil' Paws, he has redefined what music means on Sir Whiskerton's farm.

He is also, unofficially, the farm's **resident critic of imprecision.** He has been known to interrupt Ferdinand the Duck's operatic quacks to point out a missed beat. He has been known to refuse to perform if the barn's acoustics are not up to standard. He has been known to spend an entire afternoon rewriting a single line.

He is not difficult. He is devoted.

---

## Personality

- **The Lyrical Wordsmith.** He is more thoughtful and deliberate than his partner. He is the architect of their rhymes, obsessed with complex wordplay and multisyllabic rhymes. He can be high-strung, and he gets frustrated when his art is misunderstood.
- **The Serious Artist.** He sees himself as the serious artist of the duo. He does not do this for fun. He does this because it is what he is.
- **The Perfectionist.** He will not settle. He will not compromise. He will not release a track until it is ready.
- **Secretly Insecure.** Beneath the precision, beneath the devotion, there is a small, quiet fear: *What if my rhymes don't matter? What if no one hears the architecture of what I am building?* He is afraid of being dismissed.

He does not talk about this. He writes about it — in his notebook, in his rhymes, in the spaces between the beats.

---

## The Origin

MC Scratches and Lil' Paws were two alley cats with a shared dream and a stolen Bluetooth speaker. They honed their craft in the urban jungle, battling other feline crews for respect and scraps.

Their ticket to the big time was a legendary victory in the "Back-Alley Beatdown" rap battle, whose grand prize was a deluxe care package from a well-meaning but misguided human. The package was mistakenly delivered to the farm.

Tracking their prize, the duo arrived expecting to grab it and go. But as they crept into the barn, they found themselves on a stage they never expected. The acoustics were perfect. The hayloft was a natural amphitheater. A captivated audience of mice and barn swallows gathered.

On a whim, they decided to perform just one song.

The resulting sonic boom of bass and rhyme did not go unnoticed. Doris the Hen clutched her pearls. Ferdinand the Duck fainted clean away. But something else happened, too. The Valley Chicks started dancing. The younger animals looked on in awe. And Sir Whiskerton, after a moment of stunned silence, offered a single, approving nod.

They came for a package, but they stayed for the platform.

## Key Relationships

### Lil' Paws — The Other Half of the Whole

The most important relationship in MC Scratches's life. They are a perfect creative Yin and Yang. Scratches is the brains; Paws is the heart. Their partnership is unbreakable.

They bicker like brothers. Scratches finds Paws impulsive. Paws finds Scratches overly analytical. But they defend each other fiercely, and their bond is built on mutual respect for the other's unique talent.

When Scratches is stuck on a line, Paws hums a beat. When Paws is spinning out of energy, Scratches writes a hook that grounds him. They are not just a duo. They are a single, two-bodied artist.

### Sir Whiskerton — The Respected Authority

Scratches views Sir Whiskerton as the farm's de facto mayor and ultimate authority figure. He respects Whiskerton's intellect and fairness, even if he doesn't fully understand his need for quiet contemplation.

Sir Whiskerton, in turn, tolerates the noise because he recognizes the joy and energy the duo injects into the farm. He acts as a sometimes-exasperated but ultimately supportive patron of their arts.

A nod of approval from Whiskerton is the highest form of praise Scratches can receive.

### Jazzpurr the Beatnik Cat — The Generational Rival

A rivalry of style, not malice. Jazzpurr represents "the old cool" — jazz, poetry, abstract expression. Scratches represents "the new cool" — hip-hop, beats, direct expression.

Jazzpurr finds Scratches's music simplistic and loud. Scratches finds Jazzpurr's music sleepy and pretentious. But there is a layer of underlying respect. They recognize that the other is dedicated to their craft, even if that craft is, in their opinion, fundamentally flawed.

### Catnip the Stray Cat — The Territorial Rival

A purely adversarial relationship. Catnip sees the duo as loud, disruptive interlopers on his sovereign territory. Scratches sees Catnip as a grumpy, old-school hater who lacks vision.

Their interactions are a cold war of diss tracks (from the cats) and silent, menacing glares (from Catnip).

### Bigcat — The Family Disappointment

This is Scratches's most personal and complex relationship — through Lil' Paws, his partner's uncle. Bigcat represents the family's "business" of old-school, muscle-based intimidation. He sees Lil' Paws's passion for music as a rejection of his heritage.

Scratches watches this dynamic with quiet fury. He has written — and never performed — three diss tracks about Bigcat. He may never perform them. But he has written them.

## Catchphrases & Quirks

- *"If it doesn't land, it doesn't live."*
- *"No, no, no! The iambic pentameter in that bar is completely off! You're rushing the cadence!"*
- *"Wait — say that again. Slower."* (Said while scribbling furiously in his notebook.)
- *"The architecture of this verse demands respect."*

He carries a tiny notebook. He refuses to perform if the acoustics are not right. He has rewritten single lines over fifty times. He hides his insecurities in his rhymes.

---

## The Deeper Truth

MC Scratches is afraid of being dismissed.

He is afraid that his rhymes are too complex, that his wordplay is too dense, that his architecture is too intricate for anyone to fully appreciate. He is afraid that he is writing symphonies for an audience that only wants jingles.

He does not need to be famous. He does not need to be loved. He needs to be *understood.* He needs someone to hear the precision of what he is building and say: *"I heard that."*

That is why he respects Sir Whiskerton. Because Sir Whiskerton — one single time — heard it.

---

## Why He Belongs

MC Scratches is the farm's necessary reminder that **craft matters.** He is the voice of precision in a farm full of improvisers. He is the one who reminds everyone that art is not just expression — it is architecture.

He is also the farm's reminder that the fear of being dismissed is not weakness. It is devotion. It is the price of caring deeply about what you make.

And the farm, in its infinite wisdom, does not dismiss him. It listens. Even when it does not fully understand.
