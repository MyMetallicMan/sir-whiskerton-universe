## Why They Matter

Jah-Mew, Captain Swingset, and Scratch are the newest cats on the farm. They are the proof that the Sir Whiskerton Universe is not a fixed thing. It is a living thing. New characters arrive. New stories begin. New friendships form.

**Jah-Mew** arrives in a Northeast blizzard, embodying the moral that true warmth is internal. He introduces the "rhythm" of eternity to the farm. His philosophical presence clashes directly with Sir Whiskerton's need for logical explanation. He carries a ceremonial Bongo Drum and wears a brightly colored Tam.

**Captain Swingset** is a Tarzan/Pirate hybrid who views the barn rafters as the Amazon canopy. He is a master of misplaced adventure, generating physical chaos. His absolute conviction that the farm is a high-stakes jungle/ocean forces others to engage with his imagined world. He carries a "cutlass" (a bent butter knife) and has a "First Mate" (a stuffed sock with a googly eye).

**Scratch** is Catnip's bully son. He specializes in gross-out gags. But his initial attempt to establish himself as a bully is foiled when he bonds with Ditto over their ability to perform a perfect, synchronized "villain's spit take." He realizes that vulnerability becomes a strength when shared with a friend. His key item is cheap, artificially raspberry-flavored lemonade.

These three are the *future*. And the future is bright.
