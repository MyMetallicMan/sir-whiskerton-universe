# The Honorary Feline

*The dragon who believes he is a cat. And the cats who accept him.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains Longwei the Dragon — the one character who is not biologically feline, but who is considered a member of the cat society in spirit.

He is the proof that "felinity" is a state of mind, not a biological fact.

## Why He Matters

Longwei possesses the strength to level mountains and the fire to melt steel. But his true nature is gentle, curious, and deeply cat-like. He spends more time grooming his whiskers than guarding treasure. His favorite activity is basking in the sun like a housecat — albeit one the size of a small bus.

His arrival on the farm was not an invasion, but a vacation. His fiery sneezes and rumbling purrs were mistaken for apocalyptic signs. His attempts to help were seen as acts of war. His love of dumplings led to the infamous "Great Bellyache Incident."

It was only through the calm wisdom of Sir Whiskerton that Longwei was understood. And it was only then that the farm learned: **the greatest power is not fire, but friendship**.

Longwei belongs to the cats because the cats accepted him. Not in spite of his size, or his fire, or his inability to fit in a sunbeam. But because he *chose* to belong to them. And the farm, in its infinite wisdom, chose to belong to him in return.
