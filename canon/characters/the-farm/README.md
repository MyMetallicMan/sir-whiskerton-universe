# The Farm

*The core residents. The ones who live here.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the core farm animals—the ones who live on the farm, who work the farm, who *are* the farm. They are the residents. They are the family.

These characters are not visitors. They are not antagonists. They are not magical beings. They are simply the animals who call this place home.

## Why They Matter

The farm is not a setting. It is a *family*. And these characters are the ones who make it a family.

Each of them is quirky. Each of them is flawed. Each of them contributes something essential to the farm's ecology. They are the reason the stories feel like home.

They are not the antagonists. They are not the magical beings. They are the *neighbors*. The ones you see every day. The ones whose quirks you have learned to love.

The farm would not exist without them.

---

## The Three Hens

Doris, Harriet, and Lillian deserve a special note. They are not three separate characters. They are a *chord*—a three-note harmony that plays at the beginning of every story, setting the tone.

- Doris declares.
- Harriet echoes.
- Lillian faints.

It is a musical structure, and it is one of the most refined comic rhythms in the universe. They are the voice of the audience, the chorus of the farm.
