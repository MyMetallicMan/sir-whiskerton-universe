# The Henchmen

*The antagonists and their crews.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the antagonists of the farm—Catnip's henchmen, Bigcat's crew, Genghis's lackeys, and the various small-time criminals who populate the edges of the farm's world.

They are not villains. They are *misguided*. They are the farm's complications, its minor conflicts, its comedy. They are also, occasionally, its unexpected allies.

## Why They Matter

The henchmen are the farm's *edges*. They are the ones who live just outside the farm's harmony, looking in. They scheme. They steal. They fail.

But here is the deeper thing: they are not evil. They are *trying to belong* in the only way they know how. Bonbo and Grumbles serve Catnip because he gives them purpose. The Squirrel Gang steals dairy because they have not learned to ask. Bandit steals because he has not learned to trust.

The henchmen are the farm's *question*. What do we do with those who cannot believe they belong? The farm's answer is always the same: *leave the door open. Leave a bowl out. Wait.*

And slowly, over many stories, the henchmen learn. They learn that theft is not the same as belonging. They learn that the farm will accept them if they stop trying to take.

They are not the villains. They are the *lost*.
