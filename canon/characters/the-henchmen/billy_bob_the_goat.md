# Billy-Bob the Goat

**Title:** The Free-Agent Felon / The Trench-Coated Turncoat / The Mercenary of Minimal Effort

**Full Title & Role:** Billy-Bob "The Opportunist" — Freelance Hench-Caprine, Contractual Chaos Agent, and Associate Member of the Catnip Syndicate *(Terms and Conditions Apply)*

---

## APPEARANCE

A rangy, wiry Nubian goat with intelligent, utterly dispassionate amber eyes and a perpetual, skeptical twist to his mouth. He is a creature of *studied noir cliché*, perpetually clad in a battered, slightly-too-large trench coat that smells faintly of wet wool and distant alleyways. Crowning his horned head is a wide-brimmed fedora, pulled low to shadow his gaze. A crumpled, days-old newspaper is always tucked into his coat pocket — less for reading, more for the *aesthetic* of a guy who's "in the know." He chews on a perpetually unlit toothpick, his jaw working with a slow, cynical rhythm.

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

**Loyalty is a liability. Cash (or comparable calories) is king.**

Billy-Bob is not a true believer. He is a *contractor*. He doesn't do ideology, vendettas, or personal investment. He does *jobs*. The Catnip Syndicate is not his family; it's his *current client*.

He offers a specific skill set:

- **Intimidation** (via stony silence and a good side-eye)
- **Obstruction** (excellent at standing in doorways)
- **Selective destruction** (a well-placed headbutt for a negotiated fee)

His ruthlessness is not born of malice, but of *profound, philosophical detachment*. Why get emotional about a heist? It's just moving objects from point A to point B, with a nutritional payoff at point C. He doesn't give a darn because he has calculated that *giving a darn is an inefficient expenditure of emotional energy*.

---

## SKILLS & SPECIALIZATIONS

**Ambient Intimidation.** He has mastered the art of being unsettlingly still. While others panic or scheme, Billy-Bob just leans against a fence post, chewing his toothpick, his trench coat flapping slightly. His silence is louder than Grumbles's threats.

**Tactical Cynicism.** He is the syndicate's resident devil's (goat's) advocate. *"The plan's too flashy." "The risk-reward ratio is off." "You're gonna get caught, and I'm not doing your time."* It's not disloyalty; it's *professional risk assessment*.

**Selective Ferocity.** For the right price (an extra moldy turnip, a particularly interesting piece of garbage), he can execute a startlingly effective, business-like headbutt or a perfectly timed trip. It's not personal. It's *transactional*.

**The Art of the Exit.** He has an uncanny ability to vanish when a job goes south. One minute he's there, a trench-coated silhouette; the next, he's just... *gone*, having calculated that continued participation is no longer profitable.

**Garbage Connoisseurship.** His payment of choice isn't shiny buttons or fish flakes. It's *quality garbage*. A rusted hinge, a soggy cardboard box with good structural integrity, a single salt-crusted pickle jar. He views the world as a potential salvage yard.

## RELATIONSHIPS

### Catnip the Stray Cat — The Client

Catnip values Billy-Bob's cold, unflappable professionalism. Billy-Bob is the one henchman who never gets hysterical, never questions the mission's morality (he doesn't *believe* in morality), and always completes his specific, contracted task.

Catnip pays him in the choicest bits of scrap metal and expired pasta from the back of the pantry.

### Bonbo the Rat — The Immune One

Bonbo finds Billy-Bob frustratingly immune to psychological manipulation. You can't trick a creature who *assumes everyone is trying to trick him*.

### Grumbles the Mouse — The Admirer

Grumbles admires Billy-Bob's "style" and tries to emulate the trench coat look with a stolen handkerchief. The results are *mixed*.

### Porkchop the Pig — The Silent Respect

This is Billy-Bob's most fascinating relationship.

Porkchop, the eternal optimist, sees past the trench coat and cynicism. He sees a *fellow creature who appreciates the intrinsic value of found things*. Where Porkchop sees a "funky new hat" in a colander, Billy-Bob sees "viable head protection with drainage."

They have a silent, mutual respect. Porkchop will often save a particularly intriguing piece of junk — a spring from an old chair, a lens from a broken pair of glasses — and leave it by the old stump Billy-Bob uses as an office. Billy-Bob will accept it with a grunt.

And sometimes, on very slow days, they will stand near each other — Porkchop smiling at the sun, Billy-Bob scowling at the horizon — sharing a companionable silence between a creature of pure joy and a creature of pure pragmatism.

## CATCHPHRASES & QUIRKS

- *"Talk numbers, not nouns."* (His opening line in any negotiation.)
- *"My emotional involvement is a billable extra."*
- *"I don't do 'revenge.' I do 'repeat business at a premium rate.'"*
- He reads the old newspaper aloud in a flat, monotone voice, whether anyone is listening or not. *"Local Hen Faints. Weather: Unpredictable. Classifieds: Uninspiring."*
- He polishes one single, particularly shiny horn with a ragged piece of his trench coat lining.
- He keeps a meticulous mental ledger of debts and favors, written in a code only he understands.

---

## WHY HE BELONGS (EVEN AS A VILLAIN)

Billy-Bob is the farm's **necessary reality check**. He is the embodiment of the world's transactional undercurrent, a counterpoint to the pervasive, unconditional love.

He reminds everyone that not all belonging is warm and fuzzy; some of it is cold, hard, and *negotiated*.

And in his own, detached way — by continuing to take contracts on the farm — he is *choosing to belong there*. It's the best gig in town for a goat of his particular skills.

---

## SIGNATURE QUOTE

*(When asked by Porkchop why he doesn't just live in the nice barn with everyone else)*

*"Overhead's too high. Emotional rent is a killer. Out here, the terms are clear. You want a fence busted? I'm your goat. You want a friend?"*

*(He spits out the toothpick, adjusts his fedora, and turns away.)*

*"That's a different kind of contract. Ain't seen one worth signin' yet."*

*(He doesn't see Porkchop's understanding smile, or the fact that the pig has already left a slightly dented, wonderfully shiny hubcap by his stump for tomorrow.)*
