# Kanga Ruby and Joey: The Dynamic Duo from Down Under

**Title:** The Boxing Kangaroo & Her Joey / The Wombat-Fleeing Duo / The Best Family in the Outback

---

## KANGA RUBY

**Kanga Ruby** is a spirited and adventurous kangaroo who hails from the sunburnt plains of Australia. With her powerful legs, keen instincts, and a heart as vast as the Outback, Ruby is a natural leader and a fierce protector of those she loves.

Her warm, friendly demeanor and thick Australian accent make her an instant hit wherever she goes. Her love for **boxing** — a national pastime back home — has turned her into an unlikely mentor for the animals of Sir Whiskerton's farm.

### Her Journey

Ruby fled Australia to escape a mob of angry wombats who accused her of **stealing their sacred golden acorn**. Though she denies the accusation, Ruby's quick wit and resourcefulness have kept her one hop ahead of trouble.

Beneath her tough exterior lies a deeply caring soul — especially when it comes to her joey, Joey.

---

## JOEY

**Joey** is Ruby's energetic and curious joey — the apple of his mother's eye.

Always peeking out from Ruby's pouch with wide, inquisitive eyes, Joey is a bundle of joy and mischief. He loves exploring new places, meeting new friends, and munching on whatever tasty treats he can find.

Despite his small size, Joey has a big personality and a knack for getting into playful trouble — much to Ruby's amusement (and occasional exasperation). His bond with Ruby is unshakable, and the two share a connection that's as strong as the Australian sun is bright.

Joey's disappearance during their time on the farm tested Ruby's courage and determination, but it also reminded everyone of the lengths a mother will go to protect her young.

---

## THEIR TIME ON THE FARM

Together, Ruby and Joey brought a touch of the wild and whimsical to Sir Whiskerton's farm.
