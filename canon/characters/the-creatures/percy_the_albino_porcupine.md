# Percy the Albino Porcupine

**Title:** The Ivory Oracle / Keeper of the 8,000-Tile Memory / The Silent Strategist

---

## AN ALBINO AMONG THE SHADOWS

Percy is a **living phantom** — an albino porcupine whose quills glow like moonlit ivory against the farm's earthy tones. His eyes are the pale, knowing pink of a dawn sky, sensitive to every shift in light and shadow.

He is not merely a creature of the farm; he is its **living memory**, its walking archive of cause and effect, its quiet prophet who sees tomorrow's chaos in today's misplaced nail.

---

## APPEARANCE & AURA

He is a study in **deliberate contrast**. His quills are not haphazard armaments but carefully curated instruments, each one cleaned and arranged with fastidious care. They are his library, his antennae, his defensive poetry.

Atop this ivory corona rests his signature piece: a slightly battered but immaculately brushed **bowler hat**, a gift from the Farmer years ago, found in the Disneyland of Debris and perfectly fitted. It is his crown, his thinking cap, his shield against the midday sun that pains his eyes.

He moves with a slow, rhythmic certainty, a walking metronome. The air around him carries the soft, constant music of his essence: the gentle *clatter-click* of **mahjong tiles** being turned, shuffled, and contemplated in his paws. He is never without them. They are his rosary, his calculator, his companions.

---

## ORIGINS: THE EXILED STRATEGIST

Percy did not originate on Sir Whiskerton's farm. He hails from the deep, silent pine forests where his albinism made him **not a marvel, but a target** — too visible to predators, too strange to his own kind.

He survived not through force, but through **preternatural anticipation**. He learned to read the forest floor like a tactical map: a disturbed leaf meant a fox would pass in twenty minutes; a certain bird's abortive call signaled a coming storm.

His exile ended when he stumbled upon the Farmer mending a fence at twilight. The Farmer, instead of startling, simply nodded and said, *"Evening, Professor."* He left out a bowl of water and a handful of nuts.

In that moment, Percy found not just sanctuary, but **recognition**.

The farm, with its orderly chaos and gentle souls, became the board upon which he could finally play his game without fear.

---

## THE SOUL OF THE GAME: MAHJONG AS COSMIC LANGUAGE

For Percy, mahjong is not a game. It is a **sacred system**, a lens through which the universe reveals its structure.

**The Tiles Are His Lexicon:**

- **Bamboos** — the straight, growing lines of fate; the predictable patterns of sun and seed
- **Circles** — the cyclical rhythms; seasons, moods, the Farmer's lost shoes always reappearing
- **Characters** — the wild cards of personality; Doris's dramatics, Porkchop's greed, Sir Whiskerton's flawless logic

**The Wall Is the Farm.** Building the four-sided wall before a game is his ritual of surveying the farm's boundaries, its strengths and vulnerabilities. Every discarded tile by an opponent is a clue, a snippet of conversation overheard, a tool left out, a sigh from the Farmer.

**His 8,000-Hour Memory.** This is no exaggeration. He remembers every significant tile played in every significant game, which translates to remembering every minor omen that preceded every major farm event. The time the Red Dragon tile (fire) was discarded before the barn roof sparked? The day the East Wind (new beginnings) appeared when Beatrice first arrived?

To him, these are not coincidences. They are **causality made visible**.

His quip, *"Luck's just preparation meeting opportunity,"* is his core philosophy. He prepares by reading the board — the farm — with tireless attention. He sees "opportunity" not as chance, but as the inevitable confluence of prepared lines.

---

## THE ORACLE'S QUIRKS

- **Unlicensed Prophecy.** His predictions sound like bizarre non-sequiturs. *"The scarecrow's hat is tilted 3 degrees west. The tractor will not start tomorrow."* He is almost never wrong. He doesn't foresee the future; he *calculates* the present's trajectory.
- **The Ritual of the Evening Game.** He often plays solitary mahjong by moonlight, laying out tiles for four imaginary players: himself, the Farmer, Sir Whiskerton, and "Fate." Through this, he simulates the farm's dynamics, testing strategies for harmony.
- **Relationship with Ditto.** He finds Ditto fascinating — a creature of pure, unfiltered reaction. Playing with him and Sir Whiskerton is *chaos theory in practice*. *"Never play mahjong with a cat and his echo,"* he warns, because Ditto's echoing discards create a feedback loop that briefly, wonderfully, breaks Percy's ability to predict anything.

---

## ROLE ON THE FARM: THE SILENT STRATEGIST

He is **Sir Whiskerton's unseen counterpart**.

Whiskerton is the *active* detective, solving crimes. Percy is the *preventative* tactician, aiming to stop crimes before they happen. He leaves subtle clues for those who listen: a strategically placed tile by the gate that needs mending, a particular arrangement of nuts that suggests a coming frost.

His most famous axiom, born from the Great Snotty Surprise, is now farm gospel:

> *"A sneeze uncovered is a farm discovered — by germs."*

It encapsulates his entire worldview: **Neglect the small signal, and you will be overwhelmed by the large consequence.**
