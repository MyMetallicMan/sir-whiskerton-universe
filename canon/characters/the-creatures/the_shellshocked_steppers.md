# The ShellShocked Steppers

**Title:** The Slow-Motion Ballet / The Quintet of Deliberate Grace / The Turtles Who Dance Like Glaciers

**Led By:** Sheldon the Stoic

---

## ROLE ON THE FARM

The ShellShocked Steppers are a **quintet of turtles** who emerged, one day, from the shadows — a slow-motion tidal wave of new talent, ready to share the *"Rhythm of Eternity."*

Each turtle wears **tiny, custom-made ballet shoes** — one on each foot, stitched to the shell — and moves with the purposeful sluggishness of a glacier trying to locate its car keys.

They are not fast. They are not *trying* to be fast. They are *precise*. They are *deliberate*. They are, in their own slow way, *magnificent*.

---

## SHELDON THE STOIC — THE LEADER

Sheldon is the leader. He is *magnificently deliberate*. His voice is a slow, grinding whisper of enthusiasm. His eyes are small and determined.

He greets the world with a bow. The bow takes **forty-five seconds** to complete. It is *worth* every second.

His signature introduction:

> *"G-g-g-greetings. We... have... arrived... to... share... the... R-h-y-t-h-m... of... E-t-e-r-n-i-t-y."*

## THE DANCE

Their dances are *stately*. Their dances are *slow*. Their dances are *exactly what they intend them to be*.

Their signature piece is the **"Sunbeam Sashay"** — a complex maneuver involving a slow pivot toward the warm patch by the water pump. The full routine takes approximately **forty-five minutes**. It is not a *performance*, exactly. It is a *meditation*. It is a *state of being*. It is *turtle ballet*.
