# The Creatures

*The non-farm animals. The ones who live on the edges of the wild.*

---

## Purpose

This folder contains the non-farm animals—the creatures who live in the pond, in the woods, in the fields around the farm. They are not part of the farm's core family. They are not the cats. They are not the magical beings. They are simply *other animals*, living their own lives, occasionally crossing paths with the farm.

## Why They Matter

The creatures are the farm's *wildness*. They are the reminder that the farm is not the whole world—it is one part of a larger ecosystem. They are also a source of pure comedy: a yodeling fish that hypnotizes, a bullfrog who cannot stop applying for jobs, a skunk who calls himself a superhero.

But beyond the comedy, the creatures represent *other ways of being*. Leonardo the Bullfrog teaches us that you don't have to be a farm animal to be important. Tony the Bear teaches us that you can be anything you want to be—even if you're wrong about what that is. Sir Gherkin teaches us that even a cucumber can have a vision.

The creatures are the farm's *edge*, and the edges are where the story happens.
