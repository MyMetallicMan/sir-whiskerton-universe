# Fluffy the Sheep: The Walking Yarn Explosion

**Title:** The Woolopocalypse / The Craft-Store Explosion / The Sheep Who Cannot Stop Shedding

---

## ROLE ON THE FARM

Fluffy was once an ordinary sheep with ordinary wool and ordinary problems — like occasionally getting stuck in fences or forgetting where she left her favorite grazing spot.

But all that changed the day she discovered **Chef Remy LeRaccoon's "Experimental Buffet."** What started as an innocent quest for shinier fleece turned into a full-blown *woolopocalypse* when Fluffy's shedding went into overdrive, transforming the farm into a landscape that looked like a *craft store explosion*.

Now, Fluffy is less of a sheep and more of a **natural disaster with hooves**, leaving trails of fluff wherever she goes — much to Doris the Hen's allergic dismay.

---

## PERSONALITY

Beneath all that runaway fleece, Fluffy is a **sweet, slightly oblivious soul** who just wants to look her best.

She never *means* to cause chaos. But her endless optimism (and questionable decisions) often lead to farm-wide emergencies — like the time she mistook a tumbleweed for a stylish accessory and dragged half a fence across the pasture.

Despite the mess, Fluffy remains beloved by her fellow animals — partly because she's *endlessly generous* with her wool (willingly or not), and partly because *someone* has to keep Sir Whiskerton on his toes.

---

## CURRENT LIFE GOALS

1. Finding a shedding solution that doesn't involve the entire farm needing lint rollers
2. Convincing Doris that wool is technically hypoallergenic (*"It's just... extra airy!"*)
3. Avoiding Chef Remy's newest "anti-shedding" tonic, which may or may not turn her into a sentient ball of cotton candy

---

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS

### Chef Remy LeRaccoon — The Source

Chef Remy's *Experimental Buffet* is the reason Fluffy sheds like this. Fluffy does not blame him. Fluffy *thanks* him. Chef Remy is *not sure how to feel about this.*

### Doris the Hen — The Allergic One

Doris is *allergic* to Fluffy's wool. Doris has *always* been allergic. Doris has *filed seventeen formal complaints*. Doris has *never once* asked Fluffy to *leave*.

### Sir Whiskerton — The Watchful One

Sir Whiskerton keeps an *eye* on Fluffy. Not because she is dangerous. But because she is *unpredictable*, and Sir Whiskerton finds unpredictability *interesting*.
