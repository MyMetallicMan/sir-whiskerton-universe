# Humans of the Sir Whiskerton Universe

*The two-legged characters who orbit, visit, and occasionally collide with the farm.*

---

## About

The Sir Whiskerton universe is, at its heart, an animal story. But the farm would not be the farm without its humans—the quiet ones, the chaotic ones, the ones who arrive by van and leave by ambulance (metaphorically), and the ones who stay.

This folder collects every human character who appears in canon. Some are permanent residents. Some are recurring visitors. Some are one-off guests who arrive, cause a stir, and drift away. All of them, in their own way, add to the farm's texture.

---

## The Residents — Those Who Live Here

- **[The Farmer](the_farmer.md)** — The quiet heart of the Home Place. Absent-minded sage, keeper of the hearth, and the farm's unshakable anchor. (糊涂农夫 — "Scatterbrained Farmer")
- **[Martha](martha.md)** — The owner of the neighboring farm. The Farmer's quiet, steady counterpart and the story's warm, beating heart.

## How to Use This Folder

**For writers.** Each character file contains their role, personality, appearance, relationships, and story potential. Cross-reference with the animal bios when a human and an animal share a scene—the interaction dynamics are usually noted in both files.

**For readers.** These bios are written to be read in any order. They are not a timeline; they are a roster.

**For contributors.** If you're adding a new human character, follow the format of the existing files and add an entry to this README in the appropriate category. If a character doesn't fit a category, create a new one—but keep it descriptive, not restrictive.

## A Note on Tone

The humans of the Sir Whiskerton universe are, without exception, a little bit odd. They are eccentric, forgetful, well-meaning, chaotic, and—above all—kind. Even the ones who cause problems (Sammy, Mr. Wigglesworth) are never cruel. This is a soft universe. The humans reflect that.

When writing them, remember:

- **They do not know about the animals' secret lives.** This is the source of much of the comedy and all of the warmth.
- **They are not fools.** They are simply attending to a different reality than the one the animals inhabit.
- **They are loved.** Even the ones the animals roll their eyes at are, in the end, part of the family.
