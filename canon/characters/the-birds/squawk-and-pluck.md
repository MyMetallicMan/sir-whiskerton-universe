# Squawk and Pluck

*The Crew. The Panicky Conscience and the Nervously Loyal Stomach. The Perfect Foils.*

**Chinese Names:** (Not yet assigned in source material — awaiting official translations)

---

## One-Line Essence

Squawk and Pluck are Captain Cluckbeard's crew—the two scrawny roosters who follow him on every misadventure. Squawk is the panicky conscience. Pluck is the nervously loyal stomach. Together, they are the perfect foils to their captain's grandiosity.

---

## Appearance

### Squawk

The more vocal of the two. A scrawny rooster with feathers that never quite settle and a voice that cracks when he's excited (which is often). His eyes are wide and darting, always scanning for danger. He looks like a chicken who has just heard a strange noise and is trying to decide whether to investigate or flee. He usually chooses flee.

### Pluck

The quieter, more nervous one. A small rooster who prefers to stay in the background—unless there's food involved. His feathers are a bit more rumpled than Squawk's, and he has the look of a creature who has learned, through hard experience, to expect the worst and be pleasantly surprised when it doesn't happen. He is usually found within three feet of Squawk, and within five feet of any available snack.

---

## Core Philosophy

### Squawk — *"What Was That Noise?!"*

Squawk believes that the world is full of dangers, and that most of them are hidden just out of sight. His job—as he sees it—is to alert the captain to every potential threat. This includes:

- Strange noises
- Unusual shadows
- Unexpected movements
- Anything that might possibly be a predator
- Anything that might possibly not be a predator but could still be dangerous
- The wind

His panic is not cowardice. It is **vigilance**. He is the crew's early warning system.

### Pluck — *"Is There Food Involved?"*

Pluck believes that the world is full of dangers, and that the best way to face them is with a full stomach. He does not panic like Squawk. He does not shout warnings. He simply **stays close to the captain and waits for the danger to pass**, ideally with a snack in his beak.

His calm is not courage. It is **pragmatism**. He is the crew's emotional anchor.

---

## Role Within the Crew

Squawk and Pluck are the **audience and anchor** for Captain Cluckbeard's fantasy.

They are not followers. They are not sycophants. They believe in him just enough to make the fantasy breathe, and doubt him just enough to keep it from floating away entirely.

- When Cluckbeard declares the hay bale to be a fortress, Squawk asks: *"But what if the fortress has snakes?"*
- When Cluckbeard declares the pond to be the Caribbean, Pluck asks: *"Will there be snacks on the voyage?"*

They are the **grounding wires** in the storm of the captain's inspiration. Without them, Cluckbeard would float away entirely. With them, he is tethered—just barely—to the world.

---

## The Muddy Duck Incident

The day the "ship" sank is a defining moment for the crew.

When Rufus the Dog charged the Muddy Duck at full speed, Squawk's first instinct was to flee. Pluck's first instinct was to grab the last remaining biscuit before abandoning ship.

Both instincts were correct. Both survived.

Since that day, they have been Cluckbeard's most loyal—and most traumatized—crew members.

---

## Key Relationships

### Captain Cluckbeard — The Captain

They follow him out of loyalty, habit, and a shared commitment to the fantasy. They believe in him—not because he finds treasure, but because he **makes the world more interesting**. Without him, they would be ordinary roosters. With him, they are a **crew**.

### Each Other — The Bond

Squawk and Pluck are inseparable. Squawk panics; Pluck waits. Squawk warns; Pluck snacks. They are two halves of the same rooster brain—the part that worries and the part that eats.

### The Farm — The Curious Audience

The farm finds them amusing and endearing. They are the crew of the Muddy Duck, the sailors of the puddle seas. When they waddle past, following Cluckbeard in formation, the farm smiles.

---

## Catchphrases & Quirks

### Squawk

- *"What was THAT?!"*
- *"Captain, I'm not sure this is a good idea!"*
- *"I heard something. I definitely heard something. It might have been the wind, but it might NOT have been the wind!"*

### Pluck

- *"Is there food involved?"*
- *"I'll just stay back here. Behind you. Further behind you."*
- *"That sounds dangerous. Is there a snack involved?"*

They march in formation behind Cluckbeard, though neither of them knows what "formation" means. They salute when the captain salutes, even though they are not sure what they are saluting. They hold their breath when the captain holds his breath, even though they are not sure why.

They have been known to panic simultaneously—Squawk because of a noise, Pluck because Squawk panicked first.

---

## The Tender Truth

Squawk and Pluck are not cowards. They are not fools. They are **the crew**.

They follow a captain who never finds treasure, who sails a ship that sank years ago, who wears a hat that smells like mold—and they follow him anyway. Because they believe in him. Because they believe in the adventure. Because they believe that the world is more interesting when you see it through the eyes of a chicken who thinks he is a pirate.

They are the farm's **reminder that loyalty is not earned through success**. It is earned through **showing up**. Through believing. Through being there, day after day, for a captain who has never actually sailed anywhere.

They are the crew of the Muddy Duck. And that is enough.

---

## Why They Belong

Squawk and Pluck are the farm's **necessary followers**. They are the ones who remind everyone that **not everyone has to be the hero**. That some of us are the audience. The anchor. The crew. That following someone you believe in—even if they are ridiculous—is its own form of courage.

They teach the farm that **belief is a choice**. That you can choose to see the world as ordinary, or you can choose to see it as an adventure. Squawk and Pluck chose the adventure. And they have never regretted it.
