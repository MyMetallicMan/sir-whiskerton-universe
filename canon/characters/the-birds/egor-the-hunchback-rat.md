# Egor the Hunchback Rat

*The Hunchback of Science. The Quiet Fixer. The One Who Grounds the Blender.*

**Chinese Name:** 艾戈 (Ài Gē) — "Egor"

---

## One-Line Essence

Egor is Professor Quackenstein's loyal assistant—the quiet, patient, brilliant rat who fixes what the Professor breaks. He is not a bird, but he belongs to the birds. He is the "sidekick" of the scientists, and he is never out of reach of the Professor.

---

## Appearance

A white lab rat with a pronounced hump on his back, a limp in his step, and the patience of a saint. He wears tiny spectacles perched on his nose and a miniature lab coat (usually stained with something that is either coffee or experimental fluid—no one is quite sure).

His hump is not natural. It is the result of a tragic accident during the invention of the Anti-Gravity Corn Feeder. While calibrating the flux capacitor (a repurposed toaster), Egor was struck by a beam of concentrated "quack energy," fusing his spine into a permanent question mark.

He walks with a limp—not from pain, but because his hump throws off his center of gravity. He calls it *"the waddle of wisdom."*

---

## Core Philosophy

> *"You Forgot to Ground the Blender Again."*

Egor does not philosophize. Egor does not pontificate. Egor **fixes**.

He operates on a simple, practical belief system: if something is broken, repair it. If something is dangerous, disconnect it. If the Professor is about to do something that will result in an explosion, gently guide him toward a safer experiment.

He is the farm's quiet constant—the one who is always in the lab, always recalibrating the settings, always ready with a wrench and a calm nod.

---

## Skills & Specializations

### Genius-Level Engineering

Egor is the true genius behind the lab. While the Professor shouts "Fascinating!" and runs in circles, Egor quietly reads the manual, checks the wiring, and ensures the machine does not, in fact, explode.

### The Waddle of Wisdom

His limp—a result of the accident—has become his signature. The animals have learned to recognize the sound of his uneven footsteps approaching, which usually means something is about to be fixed.

### Precise Squeak Communication

He communicates in precise squeaks, which the Professor somehow understands. His favorite phrase: *"Squeak. Squeak-squeak. Squeak-squeak-squeak."*

**Translation:** *"You forgot to ground the blender again."*

### The Badge of Honor

Despite his condition, Egor is proud. His hump is not a flaw—it is a badge of honor, a symbol of sacrifice for science. He even engraved on it: *"For Science."*

---

## Key Relationships

### Professor Quackenstein — The Dreamer

Egor and the Professor are inseparable. They are the **dreamer and the fixer**. The Professor conceives; Egor executes. The Professor breaks; Egor repairs. The Professor celebrates; Egor watches with quiet pride.

Their partnership is one of the most important in the entire universe. Without the Professor, Egor would have no lab. Without Egor, the Professor would have burned the lab down years ago.

### Sir Whiskerton — The One Who Sees

Sir Whiskerton is the only one who fully understands Egor's role. He once said: *"A true quiet plan has two parts: the cat who sees, and the rat who fixes."*

### Rufus the Dog — The Superstitious Friend

Rufus licks Egor's hump for good luck. Egor tolerates this with the patience of a saint.

### Gnomeo the Gnome — The Mystic

Gnomeo believes Egor's hump has mystical powers. Egor has not confirmed or denied this.

### The Farm at Large — The Respectful Audience

The animals respect Egor. They know that when the Professor's inventions go wrong, Egor is the one who will fix them. He is the quiet hero of the lab.

## Catchphrases & Quirks

- *"Squeak."* (Translation varies depending on context.)
- *"Squeak-squeak."* (Translation: *"I'll handle it."*)
- *"Squeak-squeak-squeak."* (Translation: *"You forgot to ground the blender again."*)

His hump glows faintly after every failed experiment. Rufus thinks it's a nightlight.

He keeps a journal of all the experiments he has quietly prevented from becoming disasters. The journal is now three volumes long.

He is the only creature on the farm who can read the Professor's handwriting. This is considered a superpower.

He has a favorite wrench. It is a small, brass, perfectly balanced tool that he keeps tucked into his lab coat pocket. He calls it *"The Grounder."*

---

## The Tender Truth

Egor is not the Professor's assistant. He is the Professor's **equal**—the one who completes him. Without Egor, the Professor's genius would be chaos. With Egor, the chaos is *survivable*.

He does not seek recognition. He does not seek praise. He seeks only to **fix what is broken, protect what he loves, and be present for the ones who need him**.

He is the quiet heart of the lab. He is the steady hand in the storm of genius. He is the one who stays.

---

## Why He Belongs

Egor is the farm's **necessary grounding**. He is the one who reminds everyone that **the dreamers need the fixers**. That the ones who break things are only possible because of the ones who repair them. That genius, without patience, is just chaos.

He teaches the farm that **the quiet work matters as much as the loud work**. That behind every great invention is a quiet rat with a wrench. That behind every brilliant duck is a patient assistant who believed in him first.

He is not a bird. He is not a cat. He is not a farm animal. He is **Egor** — and he belongs to the birds because he belongs to the Professor. And the Professor belongs to the farm.
