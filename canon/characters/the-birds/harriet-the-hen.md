# Harriet the Hen

*The Amplifier. The Farm's News Ticker. Keeper of the Headline.*

**Chinese Name:** 八卦快嘴鸡 (Bāguà Kuàizuǐ Jī) — "Gossip Fast-Mouth Hen"

---

## One-Line Essence

Harriet is the farm's *amplifier*. She takes Doris's declarations and broadcasts them to the farm. She is the primary vector for the farm's news—accurate or not.

---

## Appearance

A leaner, sharper version of Doris—slightly smaller, slightly quicker, with feathers that never quite settle because she is always moving on to the next thing. Her eyes are bright, darting, and always scanning for the next piece of information worth spreading.

She carries no clipboard—that is Doris's tool. Harriet's tool is her **voice**, which is quick, precise, and entirely unfiltered. She has a habit of leaning in close when she speaks, as if every sentence is a secret worth sharing.

---

## Core Philosophy

> *"If It's Not Being Talked About, It's Not Happening."*

Harriet believes that information is the farm's true currency. A fact not yet shared is a fact incomplete. A rumor not yet spread is a rumor wasted.

She does not create stories—that is Doris's job. Harriet's job is to **move them**. She is the delivery system. She is the speed of the farm's gossip. Without her, even the most dramatic crisis would stay local to the coop. With her, it becomes a farm-wide event.

---

## Skills & Specializations

### Rapid News Dissemination

Harriet can take a single piece of information and have it spread across the farm within minutes. She moves quickly, speaks quickly, and never wastes time on verification.

### Headline Creation

She does not report. She **headlines**. Where Doris writes a full manifesto, Harriet writes a single, punchy line that captures the panic and spreads it faster. Her headlines are often slightly wrong, but always memorable.

### Timed Arrival

Harriet has an uncanny ability to arrive exactly when a conversation needs a new piece of gossip, or exactly when someone has just said something worth spreading. She is the farm's living *breaking news alert*.

### The Echo Skill

Harriet is a master of the farm's Law of Amplified Echoes. She can take a fact and pass it through three different animals, each adding their own twist, until the original is unrecognizable—but also, somehow, *more true*.

---

## Key Relationships

### Doris the Hen — The Catalyst

Doris is Harriet's closest friend, her leader, and the source of most of Harriet's information. When Doris declares a crisis, Harriet broadcasts it. They are two notes of the same chord: **Doris declares, Harriet echoes.**

### Lillian the Hen — The Punctuation

Lillian is the third note of the triad. Harriet takes Doris's declarations and broadcasts them; Lillian provides the physical reaction—the faint that makes the whole thing land.

### Gertrude the Goose — The Cautious Critic

Gertrude disapproves of Harriet's methods—too fast, too imprecise, too unverified. But Gertrude cannot deny that Harriet is efficient. She is the farm's fastest source of information, even if the information is often wrong.

### The Farm at Large — The Audience

Everyone on the farm listens to Harriet. She is the one who brings the news, whatever the news may be. When Harriet speaks, the farm listens—even if they know they should be skeptical.

---

## Signature Line

> *"Farm headline: Sir Whiskerton is bald! ...What? He's just shedding? Eh, details aren't important!"*

This line captures Harriet perfectly. She is not a liar. She is not malicious. She simply *moves faster than the truth*. By the time she has spread the headline, the correction is already old news.

---

## Catchphrases & Quirks

- *"Farm headline!"* — Her opener, delivered with the gravity of a town crier.
- *"Details aren't important!"* — Her defense, deployed whenever someone corrects her.
- *"Oh, this is BIG!"* — Her signal that a piece of gossip is worth spreading.
- *"Wait, wait, wait—you need to hear this."* — Her invitation into a confidence she will immediately spread.

She speaks in **short, punchy sentences**—the verbal equivalent of a tabloid headline. She never finishes a story. She simply drops it and moves on to the next.

She has a habit of leaning in when she speaks, then pulling back before the person can respond. By the time they react, she is already gone, off to spread the word somewhere else.

---

## The Function in the Triad

The chicken triad is the farm's *reaction engine*. Each bird has a specific role:

- **Doris** — The Catalyst. She declares the crisis.
- **Harriet** — The Amplifier. She broadcasts the crisis.
- **Lillian** — The Punctuation. She faints at the crisis.

Without Harriet, Doris's declarations would stay in the coop. Without Harriet, Lillian would have nothing to faint about. Harriet is the *velocity* of the triad. She is the reason the farm moves so fast from *"What's happening?"* to *"EVERYONE PANIC!"*

---

## Why She Belongs

Harriet is the farm's **living news ticker**. She is the reason information travels so quickly, so dramatically, and so imperfectly. She is the source of most of the farm's misunderstandings—and most of the farm's comedy.

She is also, quietly, one of the farm's most connected characters. She knows everyone. She talks to everyone. She moves through every layer of farm society. Without her, the farm would still be a family—but it would be a family where everyone had to find things out for themselves. With Harriet, the family shares everything.

Even if the everything is often a little bit wrong.
