# Artifact Bio: The Single Button

## IDENTIFICATION

**Name:** The Single Button (also known as "The Pearl," "The Anchor," or — in Auntie Clover's satchel — "One for the road")

**Type:** Gift / Anchor / Small Sacred Thing

**Location:** Tucked into the collar of Catnip the Stray Cat

**Status:** Worn. Cherished. Never removed.

**First Documented:** Received from Auntie Clover, in a small tissue, on an afternoon no one remembers the exact date of.

---

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

It is a single button. A pearl button, the kind that once adorned a fine coat or a favorite shirt. It is white, with a faint iridescence, and slightly worn at the edges. It is small — the size of a thumbnail. It is not worth anything to anyone who does not know what it is.

Catnip wears it in his collar. He has never taken it off. He has never spoken of it. He does not need to.

The button is not sewn into the collar. It is *tucked*. Held in place by a fold of fabric, by habit, by something that looks a great deal like love.

No one has ever asked Catnip about the button. No one would dare.

---

## THE HISTORY

Auntie Clover gave Catnip the button on one of her visits to the farm. She did not explain it. She did not need to.

> *"Your coat might need a friend someday,"* she said, wrapping the button in a small piece of tissue paper and pressing it into his paws.

Catnip did not respond. He did not purr. He did not smile.

But he *took* the button. He *kept* the button. He *wears* the button.

And every time Auntie Clover visits, he sits a little closer to her. He does not acknowledge her. He does not speak to her. But he sits closer.

The button is the only gift Catnip has ever accepted. The animals know this. No one has ever commented on it. No one needs to.

---

## THE FEELING

The button has no feeling. It is a button. But Catnip, with the button, feels different — even to himself.

He is calmer. He is gentler. He is, on rare occasions, *observed* to nudge a boot with his head, as if in greeting, as if in thanks.

The button is an anchor. It is a small, solid thing in a world that is often uncertain. It reminds Catnip — though he would never say so — that someone has thought of him. That someone has given him something. That someone has seen him, and loved him, without demanding anything in return.

The button is not decoration. It is *proof*.

---

## THE MYSTERY

**Why did Catnip accept the button?** No one knows.

**Why does he wear it?** No one knows.

**What does it mean to him?** No one knows. No one asks. The button is Catnip's. It is his small, private thing.

The farm has decided not to know
