# The Dogs of the Farm

*The farm's hearth. The answer to the question the cats are still asking.*

---

## What This Folder Is

This folder contains the canonical bios of the dogs of the Sir Whiskerton Universe.

The dogs are a small, tight cluster — four souls, each a different expression of the same truth: **love doesn't need to be earned. It only needs to be allowed.**

Where the cats are the farm's mirror (asking *"Do I belong?"*), the dogs are the farm's hearth (answering *"Yes. You always did."*).

## How to Read These Files

Each file follows the same structure:

1. Title & Subtitle — the dog's narrative function
2. Chinese Name — with literal meaning
3. One-Line Essence — the soul of the dog
4. Appearance — physical description
5. Core Philosophy — what they believe
6. Role on the Farm — their function
7. Personality — traits, including their hidden wound
8. Origin — where they came from
9. Key Relationships — with the major characters
10. Catchphrases & Quirks — voice and behavior
11. The Deeper Truth — the wound beneath the wag
12. Why They Belong — their place in the farm's covenant
13. Signature Quote — one line that captures them
14. Status — where they are now

## The Deeper Truth of the Dogs

The cats are the farm's mirror. The dogs are the farm's hearth.

The cats ask: *"Do I belong?"*

The dogs answer: *"Yes. You always did."*

Not because they are clever. Not because they are brave. Not because they are perfect.

But because they are *here.* Because they show up. Because they stay.

That is the gift of the dogs. And that is why they belong.
