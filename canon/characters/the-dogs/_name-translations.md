# Dog Name Translations

The English and Chinese names of every dog in the Sir Whiskerton Universe.

---

## Purpose

This file is the master reference for dog names in both languages. Every story, translation, adaptation, and fan work should use these names consistently.

## Notes on the Names

### Rufus — 发光狗 (Fāguāng Gǒu)

**"Glowing Dog."** The name is simple and descriptive. It captures his most distinctive feature — his natural glow — and his essential nature. He is not radioactive in the dangerous sense. He is radioactive in the sense that he is *radiant.* He radiates warmth, love, and light.

### Big Red — 红憨忠 (Hóng Hān Zhōng)

**"Red Loyal-Heart."** The name is poetic and heartfelt. It captures his two most essential qualities: his color (red) and his devotion (loyal-heart). The middle character 憨 (hān) also suggests a certain lovable clumsiness — the sense of a dog who means well but doesn't always land on his feet.

### Bingo — 嚎哥 (Háo Gē)

**"Howl Bro."** The name is playful. It is a pun on 好哥 (hǎo gē, "good bro") — the name sounds like a laid-back nickname a group of farm animals would yell: *"嚎哥, give us another howl!"* It captures Bingo's vocal nature and his role as a beloved fixture of the farm.

### Disco Dazzle — 闪亮·汪 (Shǎnliàng Wāng)

**"Shining Woof."** The name is built on rhythm, wink, and surprise.

- 闪亮 (shǎnliàng) — "shining," "sparkling." It captures his glow, his glitter, his golden presence.
- 汪 (wāng) — the Chinese onomatopoeia for a dog's bark — "woof." It captures his dog-ness, his joy, his simple, unmistakable *hereness.*
- 闪亮·汪 — "Shining Woof." It has a *rhythm* (two syllables, then one, like a beat drop). It has a *wink* (a disco dog named "Woof" is funny). And it has a *surprise* (it's not "Disco Dazzle" translated — it's *Disco Dazzle reimagined as a Chinese original*).
