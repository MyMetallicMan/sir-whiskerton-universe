# Guidelines & Canon Rules

*The soft rules that hold the Sir Whiskerton universe together.*

---

## About

The Sir Whiskerton universe is not a franchise with a bible. It is a *world*—a shared place with a shared tone, shared characters, and shared rules. These rules are not rigid. They are not enforced. They are simply *understood*.

This folder collects the documents that help writers and contributors stay consistent with that tone: character interaction guidelines, canon rules, style notes, and philosophical touchstones.

---

## The Documents

### Character Interaction Guidelines

- **[Interaction Guidelines for The Farmer and Martha](interaction_guidelines_farmer_martha.md)** — The foundational principles for writing the two most important human characters in the universe. Their relationship is the story's stable emotional core. This document establishes how to handle their dynamic with consistency, subtlety, and respect.

### Canon Rules (Reference)

The following rules are not optional. They are the ground floor of the universe.

1. **Nothing truly dies.** Creatures change form. They vanish. They transform. They do not *die*. This is a soft universe. When a character leaves, they do so by changing, not by ending.

2. **The animals do not reveal their secret lives to the humans.** The Farmer does not know Sir Whiskerton solves mysteries. Martha does not know Catnip is a mafia don. The comedy and warmth come from this ignorance. Do not break it.

3. **The Farmer and Martha's relationship is a slow, gentle burn.** No forced drama. No love triangles. No rushed resolution. The journey is the point.

4. **Longwei believes he is a cat.** This is not a delusion. It is a choice. Do not correct him. Do not "reveal" the truth. He is what he believes he is.

5. **The farm is a safe place.** Even the chaos is safe. Even the adventures are gentle. Nothing here causes real harm.

6. **Kindness is the default.** Even villains are, at worst, misguided. Conflict is resolved with understanding, not force. If a story or bio would cause genuine harm—cruelty, real fear, irreparable loss—it doesn't belong here.

7. **The tone is earnest.** We do not do irony for irony's sake. We do not do cruelty for laughs. We mean what we say, and we say what we mean.

### Tone Guidelines (Reference)

Tales by Firefly stories—and indeed, all canon content—should feel:

- **Gentle** — quiet stakes, soft humor, no cruelty
- **Whimsical** — the farm's absurdity is a feature, not a bug
- **Warm** — even the sad moments should feel *held*
- **Earnest** — no irony for irony's sake; sincerity is the point
- **Soft** — honoring the universe's core rule: nothing dies, things change form

## How to Use This Folder

**For writers.** Read the interaction guidelines for any character you're about to write. If you're writing a Farmer/Martha scene, the guidelines are essential. If you're writing Longwei, re-read his bio and remember rule 4.

**For contributors.** Before adding anything to the repo—a character, a story, an artifact—check that it fits the rules above. If you're unsure, ask. There are no stupid questions. There is only the farm, and the people who love it.

**For readers.** These documents are not necessary to enjoy the stories. They are for the people building the world.
