## Purpose

This folder holds the creative works made by fans of the Sir Whiskerton Universe. Stories, art, zines, animations, songs, and anything else the community dreams up.

## The Terms

Fan works are welcomed under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)** license. This means:

- **Attribution** — You must credit the Sir Whiskerton Universe and its Steward.
- **Non-Commercial** — You may not sell your fan work or use it for commercial gain.
- **ShareAlike** — Any derivative work must carry the same license.

## The Rules

1. **Fan works are not canon.** They are gifts to the community. They live here, separate from the canonical stories.
2. **The core themes must be honored.** Belonging Without Earning. Joy as Resistance. Calm Persistence. The Afterlife of Objects. The Economics of Joy. Vulnerability as the Only Door.
3. **No commercial use.** Not for sale, not crowdfunded, not adapted for profit.
4. **No claim of ownership.** The universe remains the Steward's.
5. **AI training is prohibited** without written permission.

For the full terms, see [LICENSE-FANWORKS](../LICENSE-FANWORKS) and [STEWARDS-CHARTER](../STEWARDS-CHARTER.md).

## What to Submit

If you have created a fan work and would like it included here, please open an issue or submit a pull request. The Steward reserves the right to include or exclude any work, always guided by the core themes.

## The Spirit

> *This is a place of belonging. Everyone is welcome here. Create freely. Share generously. Respect the farm.*
