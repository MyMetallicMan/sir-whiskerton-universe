# Scene Images

*Illustrations of specific moments, locations, and events from the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Purpose

This folder holds illustrations of scenes from the stories—the moments that matter, the places that live in memory.

---

## Naming Convention

`story-slug_scene-description_v01.png`

Example: `unsent-letter_farmer-in-attic_v01.png`

---

## What Belongs Here

- Illustrations of story moments
- Landscape images of the farm
- Diagrams of locations (the barn, the sunken glade, etc.)
- Splash images for stories
- Comic panels
