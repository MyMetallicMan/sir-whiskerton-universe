# Cover Images

*Book covers and title imagery for the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Purpose

This folder holds the covers of the Sir Whiskerton books. Each book has its own cover, and this is where they live.

---

## Naming Convention

`book-number_title-slug_v01.png`

Example: `271-circles-of-chaos_v01.png`

---

## What Belongs Here

- Book covers
- Title images
- Series banners
- Promotional artwork
