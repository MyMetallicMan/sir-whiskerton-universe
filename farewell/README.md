# Farewell

This folder contains the closing posts of the MM blog, preserved here as 
the bridge between the blog and this repository.

These posts are not canon (they live in `/canon/`). They are not fan works 
(they live in `/fan-works/`). They are the author's farewell—the moment the 
farm was handed to the reader, and the reader was invited to keep it alive.

## Contents

- **01-final-post.md** — The last post on the MM blog. A farewell, a 
  benediction, and an invitation.
- **02-the-day-mm-came-to-tea.md** — The moment the author entered the 
  Sir Whiskerton universe, sat in the Farmer's rocking chair, and was 
  welcomed home.
- **03-advice-from-the-farm.md** — The farm animals offer their counsel 
  to readers navigating a period of great change.

## Why These Are Here

The blog was a stream. This repository is a library. These three posts 
are the bridge between the two—the last words of the stream, now held 
in permanent water.

Read them in order. They tell a story.

Jingle on.
