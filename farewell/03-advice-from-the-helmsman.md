# To the M&M'ers: A Letter from the Helmsman

*From the MM blog. A farewell to the community.*

---

Let's not mince words.

I have been writing this blog for a long time now. I started with my 
story—the fragments, the memories, the scars, the lessons. I wrote about 
the machines I was inside. The machines that were inside me. The things 
I saw that most people never see, and the things I carried that most 
people never have to carry.

Then I started writing about what I saw coming. The changes. The collapse. 
The slow, grinding transformation of the West from a society of owners 
to a society of renters. From citizens to serfs. From democracy to 
techno-feudalism.

I warned you. I gave you advice. I told you what I would do if I were in 
your shoes.

And now, many of you have taken that advice. You are in your own 
individual lifeboats. Some of you are still scrambling onto the boats. 
Others are hunkering down out of necessity.

I see you. I am proud of you. And I want to speak to you directly.

---

## The M&M'ers

I have taken to calling you the M&M'ers. Not because of the candy. Because 
you are MetallicMan's people. You are the ones who found this little 
corner of the internet and decided to stay. You are the ones who read 
the words, felt the truth in them, and acted on them.

You are not the mainstream. You are not the conformists. You are not the 
ones who will march helplessly into the spider's web.

You are the ones who are building lifeboats. You are the ones who are 
learning to grow food, fix things, and live with less. You are the ones 
who are cutting the threads of the subscription economy and reclaiming 
your freedom.

You are the M&M'ers. And you are the ones who will survive.

---

## The Early Days

In the early days of this blog, I wrote about my story. I wrote about the 
machines I was inside. The military. The intelligence community. The 
prison. The exile. The rebuilding. I wrote about the things I saw and the 
things I learned.

I wrote about the nature of reality. I wrote about politics. I wrote about 
the structure of power. I wrote about the lies we are told and the truths 
we must find for ourselves.

I wrote about the nature of the human condition. I wrote about the nature 
of the soul. I wrote about the nature of the symbiote that lives in my 
head and speaks to me in ways I do not always understand.

That was the foundation. That was the baseline. Without that, the rest 
would not make sense.

---

## The Warnings

Then I started warning you. I started telling you what I saw coming.

I told you that the West was not going to recover. I told you that the 
economy was a shell game. I told you that the government was not going 
to save you. I told you that the corporations were not your friends. I 
told you that the system was designed to trap you, not to free you.

I told you to get out of debt. I told you to own your own tools. I told 
you to grow your own food. I told you to learn practical skills. I told 
you to build community. I told you to cut the threads of the subscription 
economy. I told you to reduce your exposure footprint.

I told you that the point of closure was near. I told you that the 
spider's web was tightening. I told you that the only way to survive was 
to be agile, adaptable, and independent.

I told you the truth. And some of you listened.

---

## The Lifeboats

Now, many of you are in your own individual lifeboats. You have taken the 
advice. You have reduced your exposure footprint. You have learned to 
grow food, fix things, and live with less. You have cut the threads of 
the subscription economy. You have built local networks. You have 
reclaimed your independence.

Some of you are still scrambling onto the boats. You are in the process 
of making the transition. You are learning. You are adapting. You are 
doing the work. It is not easy. It is not comfortable. But you are doing 
it. That is all that matters.

Others are hunkering down out of necessity. You cannot leave the system 
entirely. You are trapped in a job, a house, a subscription, a debt. You 
are doing what you can. You are making the best of a bad situation.

I see you. I see all of you. And I am proud of you.

---

## The Advice

I want to give you some final advice. Not because I think you need it. 
Because I want you to know that I am still here, still watching, still 
caring.

| Advice | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Stay agile** | The system will change. You must change with it. Do not become rigid. Do not become attached to any one way of living. |
| **Stay connected** | Do not isolate yourself. Build local networks. Share skills, tools, and food. Community is your safety net. |
| **Stay practical** | Focus on what you can do, not what you cannot. Grow what you can grow. Fix what you can fix. Make what you can make. |
| **Stay kind** | The world is going to get harder. Kindness is not weakness. It is strength. It is what will hold communities together when everything else falls apart. |
| **Stay aware** | Pay attention. Watch the system. Watch the changes. Watch the opportunities. The system is not static. It is always shifting. And shifts create openings. |
| **Stay hopeful** | Not because things will get better. Because hope is a tool. It is a lens. It is a way of seeing the world that allows you to act. Do not let go of it. |

---

## The Final Word

I want you to know something. I started this blog as a way to make sense 
of my own life. I never expected it to become a lifeline for others. But 
it has. And that has been one of the greatest surprises of my life.

You are not alone. You are not isolated. You are part of a community. A 
community of people who have seen the truth and are acting on it. A 
community of people who are building lifeboats, cutting threads, and 
reclaiming their independence.

I am proud of you. I am grateful for you. And I will continue to write, 
to warn, and to guide as long as I am able.

The point of closure is near. The spider's web is tightening. The exits 
are closing.

But you are not in the web. You are in the lifeboats. You are agile. You 
are adaptable. You are prepared.

Keep going. Keep building. Keep helping each other.

The system will fall. It always does. And when it does, you will be the 
ones who are still standing.

I believe in you. I always have.

— MM
