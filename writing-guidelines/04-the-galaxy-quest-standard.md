# The Galaxy Quest Standard

*The standard for collaborators in the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Origin

In the film *Galaxy Quest*, the actor who played the captain took the role because he loved the original series. He didn't take the job to deconstruct it, to "modernize" it, or to launch his own franchise. He took it to pay tribute. He was a guest in a house he admired, and he left it better than he found it.

This is the standard for anyone who wishes to work in the Sir Whiskerton Universe.

---

## The Standard

> The only people who may professionally touch the Sir Whiskerton Universe are those who love it.
>
> They must approach the work as co-stewards, not as for-hire professionals. They must honor the spirit, celebrate the heart, and leave the farm better than they found it.

---

## The Motivation Interview

Before any contract is discussed, any potential collaborator—writer, illustrator, showrunner, voice actor, publisher, studio executive—must have a conversation that answers this core question:

> **"Why do you want to work in this universe, and not just create your own animal story?"**

### Acceptable Answers (The Galaxy Quest Spirit)

- "I read these stories during a hard time, and they felt like a sanctuary. I want to help build more of that for others."
- "The philosophy of belonging speaks to me deeply. I see how it could apply to a new character or situation."
- "I love the specific voice—that blend of wit and warmth. I want to play in that sandbox."
- "Sir Whiskerton reminds me of a gentle, wise figure from my life. It feels personal."

### Unacceptable Answers (The For-Hire Red Flag)

- "It's a hot IP with franchise potential."
- "I have a great idea for a dark/edgy take on this world."
- "I think we can really modernize it for today's audience."
- "The animal genre sells well with kids."

**Note:** This is a vibe check. There are no scripted right answers. You are listening for authentic resonance.

---

## The Test of Belonging

The audition for a writer is not a spec script for another show. It is this:

> **"Write a 500-word scene where a new, anxious creature arrives at the farm. How does Sir Whiskerton greet them? How do Rufus, Martha, and Zephyr react? Don't try to be funny. Try to be kind."**

You are not judging their prose style first. You are judging:

- Do they get the emotional rhythm of the farm?
- Do they understand that Rufus's greeting is overwhelming but pure?
- That Martha's care is quiet?
- That Sir Whiskerton's approach is calm and observational?

### The Artist's Test

Draw the moment Sir Whiskerton realizes the sunbeam is gone. Is it caricatured rage, or weary, profound disappointment? The line work will tell you everything.

---

## The Steward Clause in Contracts

In any legal agreement, include a clause that states:

> **"The Licensee acknowledges that the Work is a sanctuary of belonging, and agrees that all derivative material shall be created in a spirit of affection, respect, and philosophical alignment with the Core Tenets as defined in the Charter of the Farmstead."**

This makes it a breach of contract to violate the spirit, not just a creative difference.

---

## The Guardians of the Farm Council

As the universe grows, formalize the inner circle. This is not a corporate board. It is a fellowship.

**Members:**
- The Founding Steward
- The Spirit Guardian
- The most trusted early editor / illustrator
- One or two super-fans who demonstrably understand the soul of the work

**Role:** This council approves any major canonical expansion (new medium, new recurring character). Their decision is based on the FCM and the Visitor's Test. Their votes are not about profit, but about preservation of home.

---

## The Love Letter Archive

Start a tradition. When someone—a fan, a potential collaborator—writes or creates something that truly gets it, archive it. These are your "Galaxy Quest Letters." They are proof of concept that the spirit can be recognized and replicated by others.

This archive becomes:

- A touchstone for you when you doubt.
- A training manual for new collaborators. ("This is what we mean. Read this.")
- A recruiting pool. The people who wrote those letters are your first candidates for future stewards.

---

## The Result

> If you only allow people who love the garden to tend it, then every new plant (story) will belong.
>
> It might be a new, strange flower (a new character like Big Cat), but it will be planted with care, in harmony with the soil and the sun.
>
> The Galaxy Quest relationship is the opposite of corporate exploitation. It is collaborative celebration.
>
> That is the only kind of guest you want in your sanctuary.
