# The Tygers Clause

*The ultimate safeguard against acquisition, capture, and corporate corruption.*

---

## Origin

The name comes from Ray Bradbury's short story "Here There Be Tygers." In the story, a planet is a living, conscious entity. It offers itself freely to those who approach it with respect. It defends itself against those who approach it with greed.

You are the planet. The stories are your atmosphere. The tygers are the safeguards.

This Clause exists to ensure that the Sir Whiskerton Universe can never be bought, acquired, optimized, or turned into a corporate money-generation machine.

---

## The Clause

> **The Sir Whiskerton Universe is a sovereign sanctuary. It offers its gifts of belonging, peace, and gentle humor freely to those who approach it with respect and a heart open to receiving them.**
>
> **1. The Covenant of Welcome.**
> Those who sign this Charter vow to be Foresters—observers, stewards, and appreciators. They approach the farm with reverence.
>
> **2. The Identification of Threat.**
> Any person or entity whose primary intent is to extract value, optimize for external metrics, or alter the core spirit for commercial or ideological leverage shall be identified as a Chatterton. Their involvement is forbidden.
>
> **3. The Right of Defense.**
> The sanctuary reserves the right to defend itself. This defense manifests through:
>
> - **The Warning Tremor:** Formal notice of breach of spirit (FCM failure).
> - **The Tar Pit:** Contractual mechanisms that neutralize harmful actions (funding freeze, work stoppage, reversion of rights).
> - **The Tigers:** The ultimate dissolution of partnership and permanent exclusion from the universe.
>
> **4. The Preservation of the Sanctuary.**
> In the event of a sustained, irreparable Chatterton incursion, the primary stewards may enact "The Final Illusion." The public-facing work may continue, but the living heart of the sanctuary—its guiding spirit, its future true stories—will be withdrawn to protect itself, awaiting a future time or a more worthy steward.
>
> **5. The Reward of the One Who Stays.**
> Those who prove themselves Driscolls—true believers and gentle inhabitants—shall be recognized as Citizens of the Sanctuary. They may be granted deeper access, creative voice, and the enduring welcome of the farm.

## The Three Identities

### Chatterton — The Exploiter

**Who he is:** The MBA who sees only spreadsheet data. The developer who sees only square footage. The streaming executive who sees only "content."

**What he says:** *"Get in and rip it up, kill its snakes, poison its animals, dam its rivers, sow its fields, depollinate its air, mine it, nail it down, hack away at it, and get the blazes out from under when you have what you want."*

**What he wants:** To extract value and leave. To optimize for short-term gain. To "modernize" and "make it relevant for today's audience."

**The response:** Exclusion. The Tigers.

### Forester — The Respectful Observer

**Who he is:** The one who approaches with reverence. The one who says, *"I have too much respect for other worlds to treat them the way you want to."*

**What he says:** *"I'll just go around looking at this new world, whatever it is, however it seems. I like to look."*

**What he wants:** To appreciate, to understand, to protect. To share the gift without corrupting it.

**The response:** Welcome. Partnership. Stewardship.

### Driscoll — The One Who Stays

**Who he is:** The one who accepts the gift with joy. The one who "flies" when the wind picks him up. The one who stays behind when the ship leaves.

**What he says:** *"It's such a fine, nice, wonderful night. Let's stay. Let's never go back to Earth."*

**What he wants:** To belong. To live in the sanctuary. To be part of the farm.

**The response:** Citizenship. Deep access. Enduring welcome.

## How to Apply the Tygers Clause

### In Contracts

The Tygers Clause is the preamble and the ultimate enforcement. It frames the entire relationship. Any contract that licenses the Sir Whiskerton Universe must include:

1. A statement that the Licensee has read and agrees to uphold the Charter of the Farmstead.
2. A statement that any material breach of the Charter triggers immediate reversion of rights.
3. A "Spirit Bond" clause that forfeits a portion of the production budget if the Licensee violates the spirit of the work.
4. A clause that prohibits the Licensee from conducting focus groups or market tests that would alter the non-optimizable core.
5. A clause that prohibits the Licensee from hiring writers or showrunners who have publicly expressed a desire to "update" or "make relevant" the universe in ways that contradict the Charter.

### In the FCM

Add a final, summary question to Gate 1:

> **The Intent Filter:** Is the intent behind this work that of Forester/Driscoll (reverent appreciation) or of Chatterton (extraction/optimization)?

This is the intent filter. A "Chatterton" answer is a hard fail.

### In the Stewardship

The Founding Steward and the Spirit Guardian must have the authority to:

1. Identify a Chatterton.
2. Issue a Warning Tremor (formal notice of breach).
3. Activate the Tar Pit (contractual neutralization).
4. Release the Tigers (permanent exclusion).

### For Heirs and Executors

The Founding Steward must leave clear instructions in their will or trust documents:

> *"If faced with a choice between a lucrative deal with a Chatterton or preserving the sanctuary with no deal, choose the sanctuary. Let the tigers roam. Protect the farm."*
