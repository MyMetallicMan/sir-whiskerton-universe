# The Stewardship System

*The infrastructure of protection for the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Purpose

The Sir Whiskerton Universe is a sanctuary. It requires stewardship to remain a sanctuary. This document describes the roles, processes, and safeguards that protect the farm.

---

## The Roles

### The Founding Steward

**Who:** The creator of the universe. The one who built the farm.

**Role:**
- Holds final authority over what is considered canonical.
- Establishes the Charter, the FCM, and the Tygers Clause.
- Appoints the Spirit Guardian.
- Approves all major canonical expansions.
- Holds the moral and spiritual authority over the work.

**Tenure:** Lifetime.

### The Spirit Guardian

**Who:** A trusted collaborator, appointed by the Founding Steward.

**Role:**
- Administers the Farmstead Compatibility Matrix (FCM).
- Protects the spirit of the universe.
- Has final sign-off on all creative material.
- Reports to the Founding Steward (or a trust) on matters of canon and spirit.
- Is independent. Not a corporate employee. Not swayed by commercial pressure.

**Tenure:** Appointed for a term agreed upon with the Founding Steward, renewable.

### The Guardians of the Farm Council

**Who:** A fellowship of trusted collaborators, early readers, and super-fans who demonstrate that they understand the soul of the work.

**Role:**
- Approve major canonical expansions (new medium, new recurring character).
- Advise the Founding Steward and the Spirit Guardian.
- Serve as the final line of defense for the spirit of the work.

**Tenure:** Appointed for a term agreed upon with the Founding Steward.

## The Processes

### The Canon Vetting Process

1. A new story idea is proposed.
2. The creator completes the FCM Vetting Worksheet.
3. The worksheet is submitted to the Spirit Guardian.
4. The Spirit Guardian administers the FCM.
5. If the story passes all three gates, it is submitted to the Founding Steward for the Visitor's Test.
6. If the Founding Steward affirms the Visitor's Test, the story is blessed as canon-compatible.
7. If the story fails any gate, it is returned for revision or rejected.

### The Collaborator Vetting Process

1. A potential collaborator (writer, illustrator, showrunner, voice actor, publisher, studio) expresses interest.
2. The collaborator is invited to a Motivation Interview.
3. The collaborator is asked to complete the Test of Belonging (a 500-word scene or an illustration).
4. If the collaborator passes, they are invited to sign the Steward's Oath.
5. The collaborator may then be engaged under contract, with the Charter attached as a condition of engagement.

### The Acquisition Defense Process

1. An individual or entity expresses interest in acquiring, licensing, or adapting the universe.
2. The Founding Steward (or their designated representative) applies the "Forester vs. Chatterton" test.
3. If the entity demonstrates Forester intent (reverent appreciation, respect for the Charter), a limited license may be negotiated.
4. If the entity demonstrates Chatterton intent (extraction, optimization, modernization), the entity is permanently excluded.
5. Any negotiated license must include the Tygers Clause, the Spirit Bond, and the reversion trigger.

## The Documents

The Stewardship System is supported by the following documents:

1. **The Charter of the Farmstead** — The constitution. The foundational law.
2. **The Farmstead Compatibility Matrix (FCM)** — The vetting process.
3. **The Tygers Clause** — The acquisition defense.
4. **The Galaxy Quest Standard** — The collaborator vetting.
5. **The Mister Rogers and Bob Ross Benchmark** — The tone and spirit.
6. **The Sacred Functions of Characters** — The character integrity rules.
7. **The Lexicon and Voice Guide** — The voice.
8. **The Case Studies in Betrayal** — Why the rules exist.

## The Legal Vessel

To ensure the protection is generational and legal, the Founding Steward should place the intellectual property rights into a **Family Trust**.

The trust documents should:

1. Name the Founding Steward and their family as beneficiaries.
2. Encode the Charter as the foundational law of the trust.
3. Require the appointment of a Spirit Guardian.
4. Include the Tygers Clause as an irrevocable provision.
5. Instruct the heirs: *"If faced with a choice between a lucrative deal with a Chatterton or preserving the sanctuary with no deal, choose the sanctuary. Let the tigers roam. Protect the farm."*

---

## The Public Declaration

The Founding Steward should publish a **Reader's Manifesto** or **Author's Note** that is included with every collection.

The Manifesto should state clearly:

> "The Sir Whiskerton stories are set in a sanctuary of belonging. Here are its laws: [list the core doctrines]. Here is my promise: I will protect the sanctuary. If you ever find that a new story violates its spirit, you have my permission to say so. You are the final line of defense."

This empowers the audience. They become co-stewards of the work.
