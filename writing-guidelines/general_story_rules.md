# General Story Rules

*The practical rules for writing any story in the Sir Whiskerton Universe.*

---

## Preface

**Please remember:** the Sir Whiskerton Universe is a **safe place** — a place of peace, understanding, and belonging. Even the craziest characters are accepted for their unique quirks, and this message must always be present.

**The farm is a crazy but welcoming place.**

---

## Story Overview

- **Format:** Short to medium-length stories.
- **Tone:** Upbeat, humorous, filled with jokes, puns, double meanings, and wordplay.
- **Story Environment:** Odd, strange, quirky, and peculiar. Weird and unusual situations preferred. Absurd situations are permitted and welcome.
- **Ending:** Always a happy resolution. The farm returns to normal, and everyone is content and satisfied.
- **Word Count:** Generally strive for a **2,400-word minimum**. If the target story is particularly fine, a longer story up to **4,000 words** is absolutely acceptable.
- **Target Audience:** Adolescents, and adults who enjoy adventure, comedy, and light reading.
- **Moral:** Includes a meaningful lesson or insight.
- **Main Character:** Sir Whiskerton, the farm's self-appointed detective and problem solver.
- **Setting:** A whimsical farm and its surrounding environment (e.g., pond, woods).

## Key Rules for Writing Stories

- **Tone:** Light-hearted, funny, and engaging.
- **Ending:** Every story must have a happy ending.
- **Moral:** Include a meaningful lesson or insight.
- **Humor:** Use wordplay, puns, and comedic situations liberally.
- **Characters:** Focus on interactions between the diverse cast of farm animals.
- **Setting:** Primarily on the farm, with occasional excursions into neighboring areas.

---

## The Deeper Rules (What Makes a Story *Whiskertonian*)

### 1. Heart First, Hilarity Second

The humor lands because the *heart* is real. A jingle collar is not just a joke; it is a metaphor for belonging. Doris fainting is not just slapstick; it is *Doris being Doris*. Every joke should serve a character or a theme.

### 2. Character Voices Over Punchlines

The characters drive the humor. Sir Whiskerton's dry wit, Ferdinand's operatic drama, Bessie's groovy philosophy, Porkchop's sarcastic pragmatism — these are *voices*, and the humor comes from those voices interacting, not from generic gags.

### 3. The Moral Is the Payoff

The laughter lands because the lesson *matters*. The moral is not an afterthought tacked onto the end. It is the *soul* of the comedy.

### 4. Tone Is Sacred

Lighthearted, never cynical. Even the grumpiest characters soften by the end. Even the strangest situations resolve into warmth.

### 5. Every Animal Is Home

The farm is a family. Every character — from Sir Whiskerton to the scarecrow — has a place. No one is *othered* for their nature. No one is *exiled*. Conflict arises from clashing methodologies, not from malice.

### 6. The Farm Returns to Normal

The farm is a sanctuary. Its fundamental peace is never permanently shattered. Whatever chaos unfolds, whatever mysteries are solved, whatever absurdities are encountered — the farm returns to a state of warm, welcoming equilibrium.

### 7. The "Visitor's Test" Applies

Before finalizing any story, ask:

> *"If I walked down the dirt road into this version of my farm, would I feel the same? Would Rufus bound up with the same joy? Would Sir Whiskerton's presence be the same relief? Does this feel like coming home?"*

*(See [The Visitor's Test](the_visitors_test.md).)*

## The FCM Checklist

Before any story is canon, it must pass the **Farmstead Compatibility Matrix**.

Quick summary:

- **Gate 1 — Philosophical Alignment:** Belonging. Resolution through understanding. Warm tone. Contribution. Sanctuary.
- **Gate 2 — Thematic Resonance:** At least three themes from the approved list, with one from each column.
- **Gate 3 — Narrative Integrity:** Character consistency. Personal stakes. Community contribution. Lexicon. No permanent harm.
- **Gate 4 — Character Integrity:** Sacred Function preserved. Evolution, not inversion. No trait inversion. No competence collapse.
- **Gate 5 — Visitor's Test:** Does it feel like home?

*(See [The Farmstead Compatibility Matrix](the_farmstead_compatibility_matrix.md).)*
