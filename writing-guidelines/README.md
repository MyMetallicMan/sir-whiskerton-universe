# Writing Guidelines for the Sir Whiskerton Universe

> *"The universe is not a fixed place. It is a field. A pasture. A garden. And you are the gardeners. What you plant, you will grow."*
> — Sir Whiskerton

This folder contains the writing guidelines for the Sir Whiskerton Universe. They are not suggestions. They are the constitution of the farm.

## Why These Guidelines Exist

In 1985, the Coca-Cola Company changed its formula to compete with Pepsi. The result was New Coke. The fan base hated it. The formula was reverted. But a trust was broken.

In the 1990s, a quiet town in Massachusetts (Milford) sold its land to developers. The strip malls and water parks came. The environment was ruined. The town council eventually stopped it. But the beauty of the place was forever changed, by the presence of an enormous strip mall and water-park.

In the 2020s, Star Trek was acquired by a corporate streaming platform. The "content mandate" replaced the philosophical foundation. Starfleet Academy was made. The fans noticed. The soul was diluted.

The Sir Whiskerton Universe will not make this mistake.

These guidelines exist to protect the sanctuary. They are the fence. They are the constitution. They are the "Tygers" that sleep in the bamboo grove—present, but not provoked.

## The Nine Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **01 — The Charter of the Farmstead** | The constitution. The foundational law of the universe. |
| **02 — The Farmstead Compatibility Matrix (FCM)** | The vetting process. Any new story must pass three gates before it is considered canon-compatible. |
| **03 — The Tygers Clause** | The safeguard against acquisition and corporate capture. |
| **04 — The Galaxy Quest Standard** | The standard for collaborators. Only those who love the farm may work in it. |
| **05 — The Mister Rogers and Bob Ross Benchmark** | The tone and spirit. Quiet belonging. Happy accidents. |
| **06 — The Sacred Functions of Characters** | The character integrity rules. Every character has a sacred function. No inversions. |
| **07 — The Lexicon and Voice Guide** | How the universe speaks. Formal diction, philosophical musing, rustic humor. |
| **08 — The Stewardship System** | The infrastructure of protection. The Steward, the Spirit Guardian, the Guardians Council. |
| **09 — Case Studies in Betrayal** | Why the rules exist. New Coke, Wrentham, Star Trek, and the Ray Bradbury story. |

## The First Law

> **Belonging Without Earning.** You do not earn your place. You arrive—scruffy, scarred, or shining—and find the door was already open. You are not granted home. You are home.

Everything else flows from this.

## How to Use This Folder

If you are a reader, you don't need to read these documents. You already know the farm.

If you are a writer who wants to contribute to the canon, you must read all nine documents and sign the Steward's Oath.

If you are a potential collaborator—publisher, studio, licensor—you must read these documents and demonstrate that you understand them before any deal is discussed.

If you are the Steward, these documents are your shield. Keep them close. Refer to them often.
